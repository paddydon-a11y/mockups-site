About Thorpe

Thorpe Industrial Roofing & Cladding Ltd is run by Del Thorpe from Spalding in Lincolnshire. The company installs, refurbishes and repairs roofs and cladding on industrial and commercial buildings, working directly for property owners and as the specialist roofing and cladding contractor for main contractors and developers.
