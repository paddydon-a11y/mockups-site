About M Buckle Groundworks

M Buckle Groundworks is run by Matt Buckle from Stainmore, near Kirkby Stephen. Most of the work is across the Eden Valley and the rest of Cumbria: farm drainage and tracks, slurry lagoons, site strips and shed bases, carried out with our own machines and experienced operators. Jobs are left tidy, prices are fair and every quote is free.
