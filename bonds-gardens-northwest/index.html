About Bonds Gardens Northwest

Bonds Gardens Northwest is a landscape design business working across Liverpool, Leyland and Preston. Every garden starts with a design for the space you have, then the planting, paving, lawns and beds are built to that plan, so the finished garden looks the way it was drawn.
