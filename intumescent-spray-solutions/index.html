About Intumescent Spray Solutions

Intumescent Spray Solutions Ltd is a new specialist business set up by Martin Webster. Martin brings experience in structural steel fire protection and large-scale intumescent coating projects, and works directly for main contractors, steelwork contractors and developers. The company is based in Herefordshire and takes on work across the whole of the UK.
