What we do

A different kind of labour company.

LabourLok supplies flexible labour across construction, security, facilities management, events, exhibitions and other practical industries.

But we're not interested in simply filling shifts.

We want to build careers.

Our initial focus is young people aged 17 to 25, particularly those who are NEET (not in education, employment or training), entering work for the first time, returning to employment or facing barriers to getting started. We operate throughout the UK and, where appropriate, Europe.
