Start as labour. Build a career.

You might be surprised where a job on site can take you.

When people think about working in construction, they think of a few familiar trades. Bricklayers. Electricians. Plumbers. Joiners.

Explore the career mapI'm ready to start

Walk onto a busy construction site and you could find dozens of different trades, skills and specialisms working together. From the people who prepare the ground before a building even exists to the people who put the final coat of paint on the walls.

And many skilled construction careers start in exactly the same place. At entry level.

As a labourerAs a mateAs an assistantAs a traineeAs an operative

You don't have to know exactly what you want to do for the rest of your life before you start. Sometimes, you just need the opportunity to get on site and discover what you're good at.

At LabourLok, we want to help people get that opportunity.

Your first job doesn't have to be your last job

Imagine starting as a general labourer.

You're working on a construction site and start helping the steel fixers. You learn what they do. You become a steel fixer's mate. You get experience and training. Eventually, you become a skilled steel fixer. One day, you might supervise a team. You might become a subcontractor. You might even build your own business.

General labourerSteel fixer's mateSteel fixerChargehandSupervisorSubcontractor / business owner

That's career progression. And it can all start with someone giving you that first opportunity. But steel fixing is just one example. You could take dozens of different routes.

You don't have to choose your career before you start it

Monday, labourer. Tuesday, hooked.

You might start on Monday as a general labourer and discover on Tuesday that you're fascinated by the huge reinforcement structures being built by the steel fixers.

You might help a groundworker and realise you love working outdoors. You might watch an excavator operator and decide that's where you want to be. You might work alongside a dryliner, cladder, scaffolder or roofer and discover a trade you didn't even know existed.

You can't always know what you're good at until someone gives you the chance to try. That's why LabourLok believes in creating pathways into real-world work.

From your first shift to your future

Your journey could look something like this.

01

Get work ready

Learn what employers expect. Turn up. Be reliable. Be safe. Listen. Learn.

02

Get on site

Start gaining real-world experience in an entry-level role.

03

Find your direction

See different trades in action and discover what interests you.

04

Build your skills

Gain experience, training, qualifications and competency cards relevant to your chosen pathway.

05

Increase your value

The more you can do, the more opportunities you can potentially access.

06

Step up

Become a skilled operative, qualified tradesperson, chargehand, supervisor or manager.

07

Build your own future

For some people, the next step might be self-employment, subcontracting or even starting their own business.

There is more than one way to build a career

Your starting point shouldn't determine your destination.

Not everyone thrives in a classroom. Some people learn by doing. Some people need to see a job being done before they realise they could do it too. Some people simply need someone to give them their first chance.

Whether you become a skilled tradesperson, operate specialist machinery, supervise a team, manage a site or eventually build a business of your own, every career has to start somewhere.

Your first job is a starting point. Where you take it is up to you.
