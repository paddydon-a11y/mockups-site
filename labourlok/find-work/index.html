Education disruption

Your generation had a different start. We understand that.

Young people entering work today experienced extraordinary disruption to education, training, exams, work experience and social development during the pandemic and what followed.

For some, the impact was academic. For others, it affected confidence, motivation or momentum.

We understand the difference between a reason and an excuse. What happened matters. But it doesn't have to determine what happens next.

What happens next is up to you
Starting again

Want to start again? Start here.

Maybe you made some bad decisions. Maybe you got in with the wrong crowd. Maybe you've been out of work. Maybe you simply decided you want something different.

We can't change yesterday. But we can try to help you change what happens next.

First job or coming back

Nobody is born knowing how work works.

We'll show you what employers expect, get you kitted, get your card sorted where you need one, and put you on a real site with real people. Then we stay in touch.

Barriers

Tell us what's in the way.

Transport, kit, paperwork, references, somewhere to stay for a job away from home. We can't fix everything, but we can usually fix more than you think. Being honest with us early is the fastest route through.

What we expect

Turn up. Try. Listen. Learn.

That's the deal. We'll be straight with you and we expect the same back. We'll remind you, guide you and probably nag you occasionally. The goal is that eventually you don't need us to.

Progression

Experience, training, qualifications, skills uplift, higher pay, a permanent career.
