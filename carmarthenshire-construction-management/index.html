About

Experienced management, on your side

Stephen Thomas is a construction professional based in Carmarthenshire, working freelance as a construction manager and consultant across West Wales. He acts for private clients, developers and businesses: one experienced point of contact who scopes the job, coordinates the contractors, holds the programme and reports back in plain English. No head office, no sales team, just experienced management on your side of the table.
