About Jan Pospisil

Jan is the only inspector at SprayFoamCheck. He is DEA qualified (ABBE Level 3), has completed PAS 2035 training and carries out inspections in line with PCA guidance. He has inspected more than 1,500 lofts. Questions after the report are free, from you, your solicitor, your buyer or your agent.

JP
Jan PospisilInspector, DEA qualified (ABBE Level 3)

No installation

SprayFoamCheck does not fit foam, so the report has no reason to say your roof needs more of it.

No removal

There is no removal arm to feed. If the foam is fine, the report says so, in writing.

No referral fees

No commission from any installer, remover, claims firm or solicitor. The fee is the independence.

How an inspection works

1

Booking

Name, postcode and your situation. No payment is taken at this stage.

2

Jan calls to confirm

Usually the same working day, to check the inspection is right and fix a date, usually within 3 to 7 days.

3

The inspection

60 to 90 minutes. Jan looks, measures and photographs. You are welcome to be there and ask anything.

4

The report

Within 48 to 72 hours: photographs, readings, what they mean and the next steps. Yours to share.
