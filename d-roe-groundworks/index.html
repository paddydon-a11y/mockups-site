How We Lay a Block Paved Driveway

01

Measure up and quote

Danny visits, measures the job and brings the brochure and block samples. Your free quote follows, normally within two days.

02

Dig out

The drive is dug to depth with a mini digger. Waste goes away by grab wagon, or on the tipper for smaller drives.

03

Sub-base and borders

A minimum of 150mm of MOT stone, compacted, then border blocks set in sand and cement.

04

Drainage

Where the drive falls towards the road, an ACO channel catches the rainwater and runs it to a soakaway.

05

Sand bed to level

Sand barrowed in and compacted no deeper than 40mm, then screeded off with steel bars and pin lines for the correct fall.

06

Blocks, cuts and kiln sand

Your chosen blocks laid and cut in at the edges, kiln sand brushed into the joints, then compacted again to finish.
