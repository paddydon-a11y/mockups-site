Planned Office Interiors was established in March 1977 and is still family run from Forge Farm Offices in Aston-by-Stone, Staffordshire. Managing Director Richard Simpson leads a team of 19, with in-house project managers, foremen and joiners, plus our own flooring and decorating divisions.

We work directly for businesses and alongside surveyors, architects and designers, on everything from a single meeting room to occupied headquarters delivered in phases. Our sister company POI-FM looks after more than 107 commercial sites across the UK.

How we run a project

Plan

A lead project manager is assigned and the project is managed to CDM 2015.

Prepare

Schedule of works, risk assessment, method statement and a pre-start meeting.

Deliver

A site foreman on every site, with daily site meetings, inductions and toolbox talks.

Hand over

Snagging report, remedial actions and a full O&M manual at completion.
