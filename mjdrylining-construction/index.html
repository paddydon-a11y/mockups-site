About MJDrylining & Construction

MJDrylining & Construction Ltd is a St Helens building company working for homeowners and commercial clients across the North West. The firm is VAT registered, a member of the Federation of Master Builders, and has won regional awards for its conversion and extension work in 2022, 2024 and 2025.

Federation of Master Builders

The UK's largest trade body for builders. Members are vetted before joining and follow the FMB code of practice.

CITB SMSTS

The Construction Industry Training Board's Site Management Safety Training Scheme, the standard course for running a safe and legal site.
