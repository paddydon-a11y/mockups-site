About the group

Four decades in the ground.

The Dockerill Group has been delivering groundworks, demolition and recycling across East Anglia and London since 1983. With over 75 years of combined management experience, our own transport division and a two acre recycling yard in Cambridge, every stage of the ground package stays under one roof.
