About

Prime construction and refurbishment, London.

C A Pavel Ltd is a design and build company based in South London. Over the last 11 years the team of designers, project managers and tradesmen has delivered listed hotel refurbishments, full house renovations, extensions, lofts and new build schemes across London and the Home Counties.

Every job runs the same way: one point of contact, a plan agreed before a spade goes in, and a finish that stands up to the property it sits in.

Projects

Recent work, in full.

Heritage · Commercial

Weston Manor Hotel

Oxfordshire · 5 star · Grade II* listed

Plan, design and refurbish 14 hotel rooms, communal areas, plant rooms, the external listed gardens and roof repairs, in line with English Heritage guidance.

  • Full consultant team provided: architects, engineers, planning and heritage consultants, M&E
  • Approvals and consents from the local authority, Historic England and building control
  • High-end internal materials matched to the client's brief and heritage guidance
Refurbishment · Extension · Loft

Arundel Project

South Woodford · London

Full house refurbishment, extension and loft construction. A new double storey back and side extension with a 4 m clear ceiling height on the ground floor.

  • New slated roof and loft construction
  • Decorative ceilings, fit out and external finishes to high-end standards
Extension · Full fit out

Pinner Project

Harrow · London

New 60 sqm side and back extension with a suspended beam and block floor, traditional cavity brick and block walls and a warm flat roof. The structure is designed to take a future first floor extension.

  • Ground floor tiling, full house decoration, first and second fix carpentry
  • New M&E and a new plant room replacing the old one
