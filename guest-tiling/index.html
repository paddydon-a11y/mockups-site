About Guest Tiling

A tiling firm that works to a site programme

Guest Tiling is a wall and floor tiling contractor based in Stourbridge, working mainly for national housebuilders on new build sites across the West Midlands. Plot bathrooms, en-suites, cloakrooms, kitchens and communal floors, priced by the plot and tiled in step with the build.

Domestic bathrooms and kitchens still get done. They just fit around the contracts.
