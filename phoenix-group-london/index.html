About Phoenix Group London

Phoenix Group London is a residential construction and maintenance company based in Orpington. We take homes from design and build through to long-term upkeep, combining structural integrity with modern finishes and quality materials. When the build is finished, the same team looks after the interior, the exterior and the roof.

How We Work

  1. 1

    Design and Planning

    We go through what you want built, agree the drawings and give you a written price.

  2. 2

    Construction

    Foundations, structure, roof and finishes, carried out to the agreed plans.

  3. 3

    Long-Term Care

    Maintenance of the interior, exterior and roof once you have moved in.
