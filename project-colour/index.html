About

Project Colour is a decoration contractor based in Helmshore, Lancashire, working on new build care homes, houses, apartments and offices. The work is contract decoration rather than a day rate: priced off the schedule, sequenced around the other trades on site, and finished so the building can be handed over. Member of the Painting and Decorating Association and registered with Constructionline.
