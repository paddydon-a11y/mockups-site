About us

A detailing office, not a bottleneck.

Steel Sculpture has been detailing steel for over nine years, working with fabricators, erectors and main contractors on commercial, residential, industrial and miscellaneous jobs.

The output is the full package: shop and erection drawings, part drawings, the 3D model and the machine files behind it. Stamped drawings when the approval chain asks for them.
