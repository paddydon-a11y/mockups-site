About Absolute

Formed in 2019, part of Absolute Group

Absolute Waterproofing Limited was formed in September 2019 out of the success of Absolute Roofing Solutions. Our team is trained to deliver commercial flat roofing systems nationwide, from two offices covering the Midlands and the North.

Every project starts with a no obligation survey and our own specification. A dedicated contracts manager produces the CPP and RAMS, sets the programme, inspects weekly and hands the roof over on completion. As part of Absolute Group we can also bring scaffolding, sheeting and cladding, pitched roofing, solar PV and building maintenance under one appointment.
