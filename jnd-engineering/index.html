About J n D Engineering

Precision steel, built to last

J n D Engineering is a new Essex fabrication and steel installation business working out of Dovercourt Bay, Harwich. We take steelwork from drawing to fitted: fabricated to your specification, delivered to site and installed by the same team, with safety, stability and regulations handled as standard. Affordable, reliable and easy to get hold of, on jobs from a single beam to a full frame.
