About RB Decorators

An Altrincham decorator that travels

RB Decorators is Richard's painting and decorating business, based in Altrincham and working all over the UK. The bulk of the work is commercial: pubs, restaurants, hotels and leisure venues that need paperhanging, wide vinyl and spray finishing done to a high standard and handed back on time. Projects have taken the team around the world.
