About

First on site.

Ollerton Plant & Civils works the ground stage of new housing developments across the North West. Plots get stripped, dug, drained, stoned up and levelled, then handed over on programme so the brickwork can start on time.

Own plant and operators, so the job keeps moving instead of waiting on a hire company. Working for housing contractors, developers and private clients across Wigan, Chorley, Bolton and St Helens.
