About Arron

Arron McFarlane is a lime pointing specialist based in Brampton. The work is lime pointing, lime brickwork and stonework, and the upkeep of older properties: farmhouses, barns, cottages and garden walls across North Cumbria. Lime lets old stone breathe, so walls dry out and the stone lasts.
