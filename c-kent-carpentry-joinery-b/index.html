About C Kent Carpentry & Joinery

C Kent Carpentry & Joinery is a carpentry and joinery business based in Carlisle, working on homes and properties up to 50 miles away across Cumbria, Dumfries and Galloway and Northumberland. The work runs from first fix to finish: kitchens, doors, roofs, floors, fences, decks and cladding, with bespoke pieces and heritage repairs made to match.
