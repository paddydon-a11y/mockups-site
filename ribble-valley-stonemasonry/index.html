About

A Whalley firm that knows its stone

Ribble Valley Stonemasonry is run by Oliver from Whalley and has been a limited company since 2016. The work runs from a single step or gatepost to all the stonework on a new build, with a lot of restoration in between: bell towers, barns, door surrounds and clock faces. Lime mortar where the building needs it, and new stone worked to sit with the old.

Nominated for the Ribble Valley Business Awards, 2019.

Projects

Jobs worth a closer look

Newton

Bell tower rebuilt at the 1842 Old Schoolhouse

Dropped and rebuilt with new stone jambs, using stainless steel dowels and ties after rusted iron fixings had caused significant damage. The original bell is back in its original position.

Houlkers Barn

An 18th century barn, raked out and repointed in lime

Full rake out and lime repoint, inside and out. Feature walls and the original buttresses are on display in nearly every room.

Fireplace install

A stone fireplace, before and after

From a bare brick opening to a stone surround and hearth. The head alone weighs 170kg.

Lytham

All the stonework on a new build

Radius copings, flagged walkways through the gardens, cladding to the building, chimney stacks and internal feature walls.

Kendal

Restoration at Kendal Town Hall

A restoration project we were involved in, working on the stonework of the clock tower.
