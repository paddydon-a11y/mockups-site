About DOC Exteriors

DOC Exteriors is a Wickford based team covering the whole of south Essex. Driveways, patios, full garden builds and the groundworks underneath them, handled start to finish by the same people who quoted it. Priced up front, kept tidy on site, and finished like it is our own house.
