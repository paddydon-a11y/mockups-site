About Methley Installation

Formed in 1984, fitting kitchens ever since

Methley Installation is a family business based in Farnham, Surrey. Simon and the team fit kitchens for homeowners across Surrey and London, and install showroom displays and retail fit-outs for kitchen brands anywhere in the country. Forty years of site experience, a BiKBBI membership and CHAS accreditation mean the job is planned, tidy and finished to the standard your customers expect.
