About Hansavest

Hansavest was founded in Tallinn in 2005. We recruit, train and place skilled and assembly line workers for manufacturing and logistics companies in Estonia, Finland and Sweden. Our workers come mainly from Estonia, Latvia and Ukraine, and work in Estonian, Russian or English.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is workforce rental?

Workforce rental means the workers stay on Hansavest contracts and payroll while they work at your site. You get trained people on the line without running the recruitment, contracts or payroll yourself.

Which sectors does Hansavest supply workers to?

Manufacturing and logistics companies, mainly in metal processing, wood processing, electronics and food processing, plus warehouses and distribution centres.

Which countries do you place workers in?

Estonia, Finland and Sweden, managed from our head office in Tallinn.

Where do your workers come from?

Mainly from Estonia, Latvia and Ukraine. Working languages are Estonian, Russian and English.

Are workers trained before they start?

Yes. We recruit, train and then place workers, so they arrive ready for the role you asked for.

How do I request workers?

Call our offers line or send the enquiry form with the roles, numbers and start date you need. We will come back to you with an offer.

Planning to add workers to your line?

Send us the roles, numbers and start date and we will prepare an offer.
