Countess of Chester Hospital

Bolt Group carried out the electrical installation to a suite of clinical rooms at the Countess of Chester Hospital, reaching handover in July 2026.

Call us Tender enquiries

Scope of works

The works covered the electrical installation to clinical and support rooms within the hospital, including dado trunking, socket outlets, data cabling, lighting and ceiling-fed services.

The project was completed by the Bolt Group team and taken through to handover in July 2026.

Package delivered

  • Dado trunking and socket outlets
  • Ceiling-fed power and data services
  • Lighting to clinical rooms
  • Testing and handover
