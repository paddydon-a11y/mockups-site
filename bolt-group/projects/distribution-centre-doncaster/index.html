Projects / Distribution Centre, Doncaster

Distribution Centre, Doncaster

Bolt Group is installing the man trap and gas detection systems serving the new freezer and chiller areas of a newly built distribution centre in Doncaster.

Call us Tender enquiries

Scope of works

The building is a new-build distribution centre with dedicated freezer and chiller areas. Bolt Group has been appointed to install the man trap and gas detection systems for those temperature-controlled areas.

Work started on site in September 2026 while the main hall was still in fit-out, with installation carried out at height alongside the other trades on the project.

Package delivered

  • Gas detection system to the freezer and chiller areas
  • Man trap system to the freezer and chiller areas
  • High-level installation within a live construction site
