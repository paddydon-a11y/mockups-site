About Bolt Group

Bolt Group Ltd was founded in August 2022 by Lewis Carr and Joel Bryan, who have 27 years of combined experience in electrical contracting. From its base in Birkenhead the company delivers commercial, industrial, residential and social housing contracts across the North West and nationwide, with a directly managed team of qualified electricians and apprentices.
