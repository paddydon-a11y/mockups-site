About Dedee

New name, known hands

Dedee Construction is a steel fixing business run by Saimir Deda out of Ilford. The company is new; the experience behind it is not. Every cage is set out to the engineer's drawing and the bar bending schedule, checked before the pour, and left tidy for the concrete gang. Steel fixing is all we do, which is exactly why it gets done right.
