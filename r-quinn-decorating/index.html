About

One decorator, one standard

R.Quinn Decorating is Rob Quinn. The man who comes out to price the job is the man who turns up to do it, so nothing gets handed down the line and nothing gets rushed to fit another job in.

Prep is where the finish comes from: fill it, caulk it, sand it, sheet it. Then the fun part. Work is mostly homes around St Helens, interiors through the winter and exteriors when the weather gives him a run at it.
