About A Dyson Drywall Finisher

A Dyson Drywall Finisher tapes, joints and finishes plasterboard for homeowners, builders and fit-out contractors. Every joint is bedded, coated and feathered out wide, then sanded so the wall is flat and smooth before the decorator arrives. Tell us what has been boarded and when it needs to be ready, and we will quote for it.
