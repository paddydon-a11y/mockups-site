About Rivett Construction & Finishing

Rivett Construction & Finishing is owned and run by Jack Rivett, who has more than 12 years' experience in construction management. The team takes projects from initial design to completion for homeowners, developers and commercial clients across Northamptonshire.

Materials are sourced through established suppliers including Travis Perkins, and every project is finished to the same standard regardless of size.
