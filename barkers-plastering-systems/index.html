Built on the tools

Barkers Plastering Systems was established in Sheffield in 2016, built on more than twenty years on the tools. The team delivers full plastering, dry lining and render packages for main contractors across housing, offices, industrial, hospitals and care homes, with CHAS Premium Plus accreditation, SMSTS supervisors and a fully carded CSCS workforce.
