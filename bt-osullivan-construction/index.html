Recent case studies

20MW Gas Power Plant, Skelmersdale

Energy · Drainage, RC structures and groundworks

The full civils package for a 20MW gas power plant at Pimbo. The main RC slab, circa 250m³, was pumped and finished through short winter days, and the drainage and headwall into the local water course were completed over two weekends to keep the neighbouring yards working.

  • Main RC slab, circa 250m³
  • 300mm to 100mm drainage
  • Interceptor and hydro-break
  • GRP headwall and gas line excavation

Gas Power Plant, Chadderton

Energy · Drainage, RC concrete and groundworks

A tight programme set by the delivery schedule for the client's major plant. All drainage was completed, tested and passed off, followed by the RC structures, the retaining wall, the main slab and the Section 278 entrance works.

  • 450mm to 100mm drainage
  • RC retaining wall and main slab
  • Section 278 entrance works
  • Site strip and tree felling

20MW Gas Power Plant, Burnley

Energy · Civil works package

The civil works package on our fifth gas power plant site. Site strip and piling, the main RC slab, a 75m retaining wall built by our own operatives to keep the project on programme, and RC road works to close out.

  • Site strip and piling
  • Main RC slab
  • 75m retaining wall
  • RC road works
