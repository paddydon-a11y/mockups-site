About Darren

Darren Hillary is a painter and decorator based in Carlisle. He takes on everything from a single bedroom to a full property, inside and out, including wallpapering and panelled feature walls. Alongside homes across Cumbria, Darren has carried out work for well-known businesses including Berghaus, and travels throughout the UK when the job is right.
