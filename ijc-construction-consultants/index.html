Clear answers

Frequently asked questions

What does the Principal Designer service include?

Our Principal Designer role covers coordinating health and safety during design, ensuring compliance with CDM regulations, and integrating risk management early in the project to prevent delays.

How do you manage construction compliance?

We embed compliance checks throughout the project lifecycle, combining technical expertise with practical tools to meet Building Regulations, Part L and other standards efficiently.

What is involved in risk management for my project?

Risk management includes identifying potential hazards, assessing their impact, and implementing controls to reduce risks, ensuring safety and regulatory adherence from design to completion.

How do you support technical project management?

We coordinate design and construction activities, monitor progress, and resolve technical issues promptly to keep your project on track and compliant with all relevant standards.
