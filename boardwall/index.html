About Boardwall

Boardwall is a general maintenance contractor for the people who look after property: estate and letting agents, local authorities, building surveyors and facilities managers. We take on reactive call-outs, planned programmes and void works across London and Essex, with one point of contact for every instruction and photos on completion.
