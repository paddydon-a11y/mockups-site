About RDH

RDH was founded in 2014 by Managing Director Rob Hayes, Warrington born and bred. Rob is qualified to NVQ Level 3 in joinery and HNC in construction, with over 20 years on the tools and running projects. Every member of the team is employed directly by RDH, and we stay on each job from the first drawings to the final fittings.
