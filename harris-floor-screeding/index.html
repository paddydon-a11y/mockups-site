About Harris Floor Screeding Group

Ryan Harris and his team are based in South Wales and supply main contractors across the region, with more than 900 homes on live developments. Ryan brings 15 years in the industry, from product and site knowledge to installing to SR1 standard guaranteed. Every traditional screed is laid with laser level technology, on projects from 3m² to 16,000m².

Fast drying screed, 72 hours drying time

Retanol Xtreme is a chemically infused sand and cement screed for time critical programmes, laid from 15mm bonded and 30mm over underfloor heating pipes.

24 hrsFoot traffic, and underfloor heating can be run
48 hrsDry to receive tiles
72 hrsDry to receive all other floor finishes

A standard screed dries at about 1mm a day, so a 65mm floor takes around 65 days. Final strength C40 to C60.
