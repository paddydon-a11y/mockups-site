About PBM Contractors UK

PBM Contractors UK is a small St Helens firm laying driveways and fixing roofs for homeowners. Residential work is all we take on, so your drive or roof is never fitted in around a commercial contract. We bring our own diggers and dumper, and every price is worked out for the job in front of us.
