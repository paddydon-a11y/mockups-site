About DraughtworX

DraughtworX is an architectural design practice at Frame Creative Space on Jamaica Street, Liverpool. Rob draws every scheme in AutoCAD and SketchUp and works alongside architects, developers, surveyors, tradesmen, estate agents and private clients. Full building surveys are available through our RICS chartered surveyor.

RIBA Part 1 Accredited

Our designer holds a RIBA Part 1 accredited qualification, the first stage of the Royal Institute of British Architects' route to registration.
