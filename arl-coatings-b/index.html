About ARL Coatings

ARL Coatings is a Liverpool spraying firm run by Craig. We respray uPVC windows, doors, conservatories, garage doors and kitchens, giving you the look of new for a fraction of the price of replacement. Every surface is cleaned, keyed and masked before a coat goes on, and every job is covered by our 10-year guarantee.

How a Respray Works

1

Send us photos

Photos, your postcode and the colour you want. We reply with a clear price.

2

Choose your colour

Any RAL or paint brand shade, in matt, satin or gloss.

3

Clean, key and mask

Surfaces degreased and keyed, brick, glass and floors masked off.

4

Spray and reveal

Fine coats for a factory finish, masking off, everything left tidy.
