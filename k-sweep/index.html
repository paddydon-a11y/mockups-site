About K Sweep

K Sweep provides road sweeping and street cleaning for contractors and commercial clients from two depots: Liverpool for the North West and Lutterworth for the Midlands. We hold a licence to use standpipes for water supply and run our own wet waste treatment facility, so swept material is recycled responsibly.
