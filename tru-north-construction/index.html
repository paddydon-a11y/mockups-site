About Tru North

Built and finished by the same people

Tru North Construction is a York building and joinery company. We take on loft conversions, extensions and full renovations across the city and the villages around it, and because we do the joinery as well as the building work, the kitchens, floors and staircases are fitted by the same people who built the room. One point of contact, one quote, one finish.
