About the firm

One wall, no joints

Steve runs God Grace Builders out of Sheffield and travels for the work. Most of it is commercial: office fit outs, retail units, stairwells and lift lobbies, taped and jointed to a finish the decorator can go straight over. Domestic work too, from one patched ceiling to a whole house. Boards go up looking like boards. They come back looking like one wall.

How it runs

Four passes and it is gone

01

Board check

Walk the job, check the boarding and flag anything that needs fixing first.

02

Tape and bed

Tape bedded into every joint, internals and externals beaded up.

03

Fill and feather

Two coats wider than the last so the joint runs out to nothing.

04

Sand and hand over

Sanded flat, dust cleared and handed to the decorators.
