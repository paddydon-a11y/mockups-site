Why it exists

A new bathroom looks perfect on handover day. Years two to five are when the small things fail.

And in a bathroom, a small thing upstairs becomes a big thing in the ceiling below. These are the five places water gets in first. Once a year, we check every one of them.

01

Silicone

Sealant shrinks, blackens and pulls away from the bath and tray edge. Once it lets go, every shower sends water behind the panel where nobody can see it.

  • Every run inspected at bath, tray, basin and worktop
  • Failed runs cut out and re-sealed on the day
  • Mould-resistant sanitary silicone, tooled to the same finish we fit with

Left alone: rotten boards, black mould, a stained ceiling downstairs.
02

Flexi hoses

The braided hoses under your basin, WC and bath tap sit at mains pressure every hour of every day. The rubber inside perishes long before the outside looks tired.

  • Every flexi inspected for bulging, corrosion and kinks
  • Perished hoses replaced there and then
  • Isolation valves worked and tested so they shut off when you need them
Basin flexiCheck
Left alone: one of the most common causes of escape-of-water insurance claims.
03

Pipework & wastes

Compression joints weep a drop at a time. Traps slow, overflows block, and valves seize so they will not shut off in an emergency.

  • Visible pipework and joints checked for weeps and movement
  • Waste traps and overflows cleared and tested
  • Cistern fill valve, flush and overflow checked
Waste trapCheck
Left alone: a damp vanity base, a smell you cannot trace, no shut-off when it matters.
04

Screens, seals & grout

Shower screen seals harden and gap. Grout cracks at the corners. Panel joints open a hair's width. Water finds all of it.

  • Screen seals, hinges and fixings checked and adjusted
  • Grout and tile edges checked for cracks and hollow spots
  • Tray and wetroom floor checked for fall, drainage and movement

05

Lighting & extraction

The glow is what you paid for. LED drivers, backlit mirrors and extractor fans fail quietly, and a bathroom that does not clear its steam is a bathroom growing mould.

  • Extractor running, clearing and on timer
  • LED coves, backlit mirrors and drivers checked
  • Heated towel rail bled, valves checked

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you cover bathrooms you did not fit?

Yes. Any bathroom, any age. The only difference is that we survey it first. If it needs work to bring it up to a standard we are happy to stand behind, we quote that separately, and once it is done you join the plan at the same price as everyone else.

Is the initial survey free?

If you had your bathroom from us there is no survey, you go straight on. For bathrooms fitted by someone else the survey is a full inspection with a written report, and we will confirm the survey fee when you book. If the room passes with nothing to fix, you go straight onto the plan.

What counts as a fix done on the day?

Cutting out and re-sealing a failed run of silicone, tightening a weeping joint, adjusting a screen seal or hinge, bleeding a towel rail, replacing a perished flexi. Anything that needs parts ordered, a second visit, or opening up tiling goes in your report with a price first.

Is this insurance?

No. It is a maintenance and inspection plan from the people who fit bathrooms. It does not replace your home insurance. It is there to stop the leak that would have you calling your insurer, and to give you a dated, photographed record if you ever do.

What if something goes wrong between visits?

Call us. Members get priority booking and repair work is quoted at the member rate. If it is something we fixed or replaced at your last inspection, we put it right.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Monthly by Direct Debit. Terms are set out in your plan agreement before you sign up, so you know exactly what you are committing to.

Where do you cover?

Glasgow and the surrounding areas as standard, and across Scotland for bathrooms we fitted. Ask us about anywhere else.
