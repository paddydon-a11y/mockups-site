Straight answers

The questions we get every week

How does this work with retentions?
Retentions, usually 2.5% to 5% held until practical completion or the end of the defects period, reduce the amount available to fund. We check that with you upfront rather than after the fact. Selective finance is built for the gap between raising an application for payment and it clearing your bank, not as a way round retention terms.
Do I have to fund every invoice?
No. That is the whole point of a selective facility. You pick the invoices you want funded and leave the rest of your sales ledger where it is. Fund one this quarter, five the next, or none at all.
What does it cost?
There are no set-up costs, no arrangement fees, no early repayment charges and no termination fees. You only pay for the funds you actually use, and the cost of each advance is quoted to you before you commit to it.
Is the decision based on my credit history?
Approval rests mainly on the creditworthiness of the customer who owes you the money, rather than on your own borrowing history. The advance is secured against the invoice itself.
My contract restricts assignment. Does that stop us?
Some standard-form construction contracts include clauses restricting assignment of payment, so it is worth checking your terms before you apply. Send them over and we will tell you honestly whether the facility fits before anyone spends time on an application.
How quickly can funds actually arrive?
Once an invoice is approved and verified, funds go out by same-day faster payment at no additional cost, typically landing within 48 hours of approval. Facilities run from £25,000 to £250,000.
