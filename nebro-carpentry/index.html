About

One carpenter, start to finish

Nebro Carpentry is a one man carpentry business based in Birmingham, three years on the tools and working up to 30 miles out. Most weeks it is slat walls, media walls and fitted storage, with doors, skirting and studwork in between. Same fitter from set out to the last bead of caulk, and the room swept before we leave.
