About TLC Scaffolding

TLC Scaffolding was established in 2022 by director Leigh Carroll, a working scaffolder who leads on site. The business has grown from domestic roofing jobs into commercial refurbishment, industrial access and bespoke event structures across South Wales, built on turning up when agreed, clear quotations and respect for every site.
