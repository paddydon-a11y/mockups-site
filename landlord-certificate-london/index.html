Landlord safety questions

Not sure what your property needs? Ask the office and we will tell you straight.

Gas safety checks must be carried out at least once every 12 months. Electrical installations in privately rented homes need an EICR at least every five years. An EPC lasts ten years.

It depends on the size of the property and how many appliances there are. On average a gas safety inspection takes around two to three hours, and an electrical inspection around three to four hours.
