A family run fit out contractor

Planned Office Interiors was established in March 1977 and is still family run from Forge Farm Offices in Aston-by-Stone. Managing Director Richard Simpson leads a team of 19, with in-house project managers, foremen and joiners, plus our own flooring and decorating divisions. Our sister company POI-FM looks after more than 107 commercial sites across the UK.
