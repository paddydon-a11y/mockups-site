About Leidon

Our own plant, our own people, since 2015

Leidon was founded in May 2015 and has grown year on year on repeat work from main contractors. We invest in new machinery, vehicles and tools, and every site team carries a tablet linked to the company server, so drawings, inspection and test records are live on site and in the office.

QualityInspection and test records kept for every package.
