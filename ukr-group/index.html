About us

Expanding the construction talent pool since 2013

UKR was founded by Darragh O’Brien and Michael Hegarty and specialises in temporary, contract and permanent placements across Construction, Civil Engineering, Rail & Utilities and Logistics Management. Our recruiters have deep knowledge of their chosen fields and excel at interpreting current trends to map the best route forward.

There is no such thing as a one-size-fits-all approach at UKR Group. We build exceptional relationships with candidates and clients and adapt our solutions to meet their individual needs.

Our leadership team are hands on, day to day, to stay at the front of market trends. We operate in an open, transparent and accountable way, agree realistic and achievable outcomes and, if there is a problem, we own it along with the solutions.

Our seven-stage vetting process

Every worker is checked for eligibility to work in the UK, competence, and the qualifications and certifications needed for the job. They arrive with the correct approved PPE and tools, fit for work and on time.

  1. 01Online application and registration
  2. 02Review of CVs and tickets
  3. 03Phone interview
  4. 04Document check
  5. 05Visa check
  6. 06Referees contacted
  7. 07Full induction at our premises where needed
