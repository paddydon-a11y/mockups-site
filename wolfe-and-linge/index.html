About Wolfe & Linge

Wolfe & Linge Bespoke Landscapes is a family-run landscaping company based in Norwich, founded by company directors Lewis and Aaron. Our team of skilled, experienced professionals handles each project from the initial consultation and design through to the build, using premium materials throughout. Our presence at the Chelsea Flower Show reflects the standard we work to on every garden.
