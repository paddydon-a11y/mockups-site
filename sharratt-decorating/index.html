About

Careful work, start to finish

Sharratt Decorating is run by Anna, a Manchester decorator who takes on painting, decorating and plastering across the city and the towns around it. Interior or exterior, one room or a whole house, the prep gets the same attention as the paint.

Furniture covered, edges cut in by hand, and the place left clean at the end of every day. Every job starts with a free written quote so you know exactly where you stand before anything begins.
