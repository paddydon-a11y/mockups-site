Why come direct

Two ways to get the same bedroom fitted.

The usual route

Through the retailer

  1. 1You pay the retailer's installation charge with your order.
  2. 2The retailer books a subcontract fitter to do the job.
  3. 3The fitter receives a share of what you paid.
The direct route

Straight to the fitter

  1. 1You buy the bedroom you want, from whoever you like.
  2. 2We give you one fixed fitting price before anything is booked.
  3. 3The same experienced fitters install it, and you pay us direct.
