About

22 years at the stone

Bryce Millen is a stonemason based in Cambuslang, working across Glasgow and the surrounding area for 22 years. The bread and butter is stone cleaning and restoration, with pillars a particular speciality: cleaned, rebuilt and re-capped to stand another hundred years.

One tradesman, one standard. Every job quoted honestly and finished to last.
