About Define

Define is a commercial fit out contractor based in Rochester, Kent, founded in November 2019. Every project is managed in house by our own project managers, working with our site managers from pre-construction through to handover. We completed our 250th project in January 2023 and launched in the UAE in January 2025.

Define delivers excellence through rigorous quality standards and experienced project leadership at every stage of delivery.Ollie Barnett, Managing Director

SMASWorksafe accredited
  • RAMS for every project. Risk assessments and method statements are produced by our in house safety advisor after a site visit, then read, dated and signed by every operative.
  • Carded operatives. All site operatives hold a valid CSCS card, asbestos awareness and manual handling certificates.
  • Independent support. Lighthouse Safety Ltd supports our health and safety team throughout the year, alongside our SMAS accreditation.
  • Permit to work. Daily pre-start inspections, weekly toolbox talks and permits for hot works and work at height.
