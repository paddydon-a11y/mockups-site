Who you are dealing with

Five men, and the same five men.

JM Builders White is a five-man outfit working out of Bedfordshire, with fifty years in the trade behind it. Most of the work is commercial: shop and salon units taken from bare shell to trading, then kept right afterwards on repairs and maintenance. The rest is domestic, and it gets the same five men.
