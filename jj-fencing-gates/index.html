About JJ Fencing & Gates

JJ Fencing & Gates is a fencing firm based in Southport. We build our featheredge panels ourselves in our own workshop, then fit them on slotted concrete posts and gravel boards, so the panel that goes up in your garden is one we made. Gates are made to fit the opening, and repairs are matched to the fence already there.
