About J&L

A Surrey roofer you can get hold of

J&L is run by James McGinn out of Smallfield, just outside Horley. Reliable, honest and highly skilled roofers offering the complete roofing service, from a major re-roof down to a slipped tile or a blocked gutter. Every job is guaranteed and priced from a written estimate before work starts, and there are no upfront payments: you pay when the work is finished and you are happy with it.
