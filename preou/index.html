About A.C. Preou

Three generations of plant rooms, and no agency labour

Arthur Preou started the company in 1961 with two employees, as plumbing and heating contractors to builders around Hornchurch. Approved status with the local councils followed, then the hospital boards and government bodies, and the firm became a limited company in 1964. Today the company is still family run from Essex and Arthur is still at the helm.

We train our own engineers through a BEST managed apprentice programme and never use agency labour. Estimating is computerised, drawings are produced in AutoCAD and programmes are planned in ASTA, so a boiler house can be prefabricated in our workshop and installed in a school holiday.

Case studies

Live buildings, tight programmes, no plant down time

01

St Pancras Chambers

Grade I listed · Historic buildings

Mechanical services modernisation of the penthouse suite in the three turreted upper floors. No heat or spark generating tools allowed under the Listed Buildings Act, so plumbing went in with press fit fittings. The twin pumped booster and sectional cold water tanks were dismantled and rebuilt piece by piece in the plant room, with a full leak detection system to an alarm panel.

  • Press fit pipework
  • Sectional tanks
  • Leak detection
  • Client: Firmco Ltd
02

Blackshots Leisure Centre

£400k · Leisure and public buildings

Old boiler out, 1100kW Potterton Arizona in, plus a new sectional cold tank, fast recovery hot water cylinder and Dantherm air handling for the pool hall. A new changing village needed pumped showers, underfloor heating and heat reclaim ventilation. The centre stayed open throughout, sequenced on our PowerProject planning and a new BMS linked to the client's offices.

  • 1100kW boiler
  • Pool ventilation
  • Underfloor heating
  • Kept operational
03

Lea Bridge Road

Hackney Council · 56 flats

Faulty boilers serving 56 flats replaced for Hackney Council, with the kitchen and roof extract fans modified while we were there. Residents kept their heating and hot water for the whole job on our own 120kW commercial temporary heating system.

  • Boiler replacement
  • Extract fans
  • 120kW temporary heating
