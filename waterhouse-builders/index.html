About Waterhouse Builders

Forty years on the Fylde Coast

Waterhouse Builders is a family run business, established in 1981 and still working out of Blackpool. The projects have grown with it: developer schemes, large residential new builds and substantial extensions, carried out by an experienced and well qualified team of tradesmen. Listen to what the client wants, price it honestly, finish on time and to budget.
