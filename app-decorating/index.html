Painting & Decorating

Our core trade. Interior and exterior painting and decorating for homes and small commercial properties, with the prep done right so the finish lasts.

  • Walls, ceilings & woodwork
  • Masonry & render paint
  • Doors, windows & fascias
  • Wallpaper hanging
  • Filling, sanding & repairs
  • Full house or single room

Interior Decorating

Living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, halls and stairs. Clean lines, tidy edges, and your furniture covered and put back.

Exterior Decorating

Masonry paint, render, timber and metalwork. Protects the building and lifts the whole street.

House Wash Downs

Pure water, water-fed pole cleaning of render, UPVC, windows, doors and conservatories. Streak-free and no harsh chemicals.

Gutter Clearing

Gutters vacuumed clear from the ground, downpipes checked, before blocked water damages your render and brickwork.

Exterior Property Maintenance

Roof lanterns, conservatory roofs, fascias and soffits cleaned and kept in good order year round.
