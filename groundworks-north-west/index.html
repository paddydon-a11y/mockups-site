About Groundworks North West

We are a groundworks and civils contractor based in Buckley, Flintshire, working for housebuilders, main contractors, local authorities and play equipment manufacturers. We run dedicated road and sewer adoption teams, a play area team and our own plant, and we liaise with United Utilities, Welsh Water, Severn Trent and local highways authorities on our clients' behalf.
