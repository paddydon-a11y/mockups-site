About Hoselines Scotland

Hoselines Scotland is run by Michael Orr from Perth. When a hose lets go on a digger, a tractor or a production line, you speak to Michael himself and get the right hose and fittings for the machine. Contractors, farmers and engineering businesses across Perthshire and beyond have one local contact for hydraulic hose enquiries.
