About Tri-Mech

Mechanical engineers who are also sprinkler installers

Tri-Mech Ltd is a Sittingbourne mechanical contractor: commercial and domestic gas, commercial heating, full plant and tank rooms, across Kent and London. Our engineers are dual-skilled, so the team hanging your heating pipework can install and survey the sprinklers too. Fire protection runs through our sister company, Tri-Fire Protection Ltd, engineers with fifteen-plus years on sprinklers and risers.
