What we do

Survey and site engineering, start to handover.

Topographical Surveys

Contours, levels and fine detail, delivered as a digital terrain model or a 2D plan and referenced to OS. The survey your architect and engineer both design from.

Measured Building Surveys

Floor plans, elevations and sections. Laser scanned, any size of building.

Setting Out & Site Engineering

Site control and setting out. Engineers by the visit or placed permanently.

Movement Monitoring

Designed, installed and run. New build, heritage, bridges and retaining walls.

As-Built Surveys

Lift shaft verticality, slab levels and finished works checked against tolerance.

Volumetric Surveys

Cut and fill, bulk earthworks and stockpile volumes, accurately quantified.

UAV & Drone Surveys

Qualified pilots and a full fleet. Aerial photography or a complete site scan.

Machine Control

Models built and loaded so your plant works straight to the design.

GIA & Area Reports

Gross internal areas and AMR reports on units, blocks and high rises.

GPR Surveys

Ground penetrating radar to locate buried services before you break ground.

CAD Draughting & Modelling

Survey data turned into clean drawings and models, in the format you work in.

Who we are

Geospatial engineers, not a hire desk.

Landpoint Surveying is a geospatial engineering company based in London and working across the UK. We provide surveying, site engineering, as-builts, monitoring and modelling to clients of every size and sector, from a single plot topo to area reports on high rise blocks. Our engineers are CSCS carded and our drone pilots are fully qualified.
