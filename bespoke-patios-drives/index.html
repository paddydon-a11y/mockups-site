About us

The half you never see.

Bespoke Patios & Drives is a Bromley based paving outfit working right across London. Drives, patios, steps, drainage and the groundwork underneath them.

Most of a paving job is the part nobody looks at: the dig, the depth of sub-base, the compaction and the falls. Get that right and the surface stays flat and drains for years. Get it wrong and no amount of nice block hides it.
