About RC Fabrications

If it is made of steel, we can make it

RC Fabrications is a steel fabrication and welding workshop in Northampton. We cut, fold, weld and finish mild and stainless steel for plant hire firms, groundworkers, builders and private customers. Most of what leaves the workshop is a lifting skip, a site cage or a plant part. If you can draw it, we can build it.
