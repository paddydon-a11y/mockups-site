Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are needed for a repatriation?

We need the death certificate and passport of the deceased, and your own contact details. Our team takes care of every other document, including translations where they are required.

How long does a repatriation take?

We aim to complete an international repatriation in 5 to 10 working days. The time begins from the date the deceased is released by the local authorities.

How much does a repatriation cost?

The cost depends on the country and the arrangements you choose, and we aim to keep it as low as possible. A full repatriation service from the UK starts from £1,800 to Ireland, £2,300 to India and £2,400 to the USA. Please call us for a quote.

Can we hold a service before the repatriation?

Yes. We can arrange a memorial service in our Chapel of Rest, a church or another faith venue for family and friends before your loved one travels.

Can ashes be repatriated instead?

Yes. We can arrange a local cremation and repatriate the ashes to their destination. Cremation is available in many countries, but not all, so please contact us to discuss your situation.

Do you work with funeral directors overseas?

Yes. We work with funeral partners all over the world through a network of over 500 agents, selected for their reputation and the quality of service they offer.
