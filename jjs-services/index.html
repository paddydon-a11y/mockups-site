About the work

One job, done the same way every time.

The bath comes out, the walls get panelled and a level tray goes in where you used to climb over the side. Panels instead of tiles means no grout to scrub and nothing sitting there waiting to leak. Seats and grab rails go wherever you want them, not wherever the book says.

Not ready for a full conversion?

Dripping tap, shower that has given up, cistern running on all night. Those are jobs too. Give Jamie a ring or send a message and he will come and have a look.
