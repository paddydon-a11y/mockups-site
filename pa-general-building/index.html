About P&A

One team, every stage of the job

P&A General Building Services Ltd is a Wembley building company working across North West London. We take on the structural side most builders sub out, steels, joists, dormers and flat roofs, and carry the job through to tiling, plastering and the last coat of paint.

Homeowners get one point of contact from strip out to hand over. Landlords, letting agents and commercial clients get the same team on every visit.
