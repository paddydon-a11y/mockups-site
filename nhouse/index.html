What we do

Come in at any stage

Most clients start with a question, not a build. We work at whatever point you have reached.

Stage 01

Consultancy

Honest early advice on whether a plot, a budget and an idea actually work together.

  • Feasibility and budget reality
  • Plot finding and site assessment
  • Route to planning permission
  • Funding and mortgage support
Stage 02

Design for manufacture

Turning a design into something that can be built offsite, to cost and to programme.

  • Design development and specification
  • Structural and warranty approvals
  • Building control sign off
  • Foundation and service design
Stage 03

Full build

Site setup to handover, with one team accountable for the programme and the budget.

  • Demolition, clearance and groundworks
  • Offsite manufacture and erection
  • Utility and service connections
  • Fit out, commissioning and keys
About nHouse

A partner who has done this before

nHouse was founded in 2017 to make building your own home simpler than it usually is. We have grown into a consultancy led business, because the moment a client needs us most is often long before there is anything to build.

Every home is different, so we work to the design brief in front of us. Modular is the method, not the limit.

Projects

Completed and in progress

Harpenden

Hertfordshire . Three house modular scheme

Three homes on one site, manufactured offsite and erected in weeks rather than months. Dark vertical cladding over a rendered base keeps the street elevation quiet, while the upper floors carry the volume.

The scheme was handled as a single programme: groundworks, foundations, manufacture, erection, services and fit out, with one point of contact throughout.

  • HomesThree
  • MethodOffsite modular
  • ScopePlot to handover

The Cotswolds

Gloucestershire . Bespoke single home

A house that had to sit in open countryside without shouting about it. Timber cladding, a simple pitched form and a long view down the valley, delivered to a fully bespoke brief.

Projects like this are the answer to the question we are asked most: can modular look like this. It can, because the design comes first and the method follows it.

  • BriefFully bespoke
  • SettingOpen countryside
  • FinishTimber and zinc
Process

What working with us looks like

01

First conversation

What you want to build, what you have, and what it is likely to cost. No obligation either way.

02

Plot and feasibility

Site assessment, constraints, access and services. We tell you early if a plot will not work.

03

Design

Your brief developed into a design, with the budget tracked alongside it from the start.

04

Planning

Submission, consultation and conditions handled, with drawings prepared for approval.

05

Approvals and funding

Building control, structural warranty and self-build mortgage stages lined up in order.

06

Manufacture

The house is built offsite while groundworks and foundations go in on site.

07

Erection and services

Modules craned in and made watertight, then utilities and connections completed.

08

Fit out and handover

Internal finishes, commissioning, sign off and the keys in your hand.

Not every client needs all eight stages. Some come to us at stage one and stay to the end, others only want help getting through planning.
