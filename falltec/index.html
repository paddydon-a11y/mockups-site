Frequently asked questions

How often do I need my safety systems inspected?

Inspection frequency depends on the type of fall protection system, its intended use, manufacturer requirements and the applicable standards or regulations. Many permanently installed fall protection systems require periodic inspection at intervals not exceeding 12 months, while some equipment may require more frequent examination. Falltec can review your assets and advise on the appropriate inspection requirements.

What happens if a system is damaged?

If damage, deterioration or a defect is identified during an inspection, it will be clearly recorded within our report together with the recommended action. Where appropriate, Falltec can provide remedial works, replacement components or further technical investigation to help return the system to a safe and compliant condition.

How does Falltec maintain quality and consistency?

Falltec maintains quality through competent inspection, clear documentation and consistent reporting procedures. Inspection findings are recorded with supporting evidence, giving clients a clear understanding of the condition and compliance status of their assets.

What areas do you cover?

Falltec provides fall protection services throughout the UK from our base in Stoke-on-Trent, Staffordshire.
