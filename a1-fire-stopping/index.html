About A1

A1 Fire Stopping & Drilling Services Ltd was established in Ashington in 2016 as a specialist passive fire protection contractor. We work with main contractors, developers and building owners on commercial and construction projects. Fire stopping, fire door works, cavity barriers, intumescent coatings and drilling are carried out by one company, so a single team is accountable for every opening it forms and seals.
