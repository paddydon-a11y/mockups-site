About

Meet Eddie

Eddie Wireworks is run by Eddie, a London electrician who got tired of telling customers to find someone else for the plumbing and the building work. So he stopped. Electrical installations come first, but bathrooms, pipework and full house extensions are all handled under the same roof. Sole trader, which means the person who quotes your job is the person who turns up to do it.
