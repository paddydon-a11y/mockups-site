About Astute Engineering

Astute Engineering is led by Anthony Brash, who brings nearly 23 years of experience in the mechanical pipework industry. The company delivers specialist pipework and engineered solutions for main contractors and industrial clients, and has launched Astute Offsite, a dedicated fabrication division, so more of each project can be built off site and installed faster.
