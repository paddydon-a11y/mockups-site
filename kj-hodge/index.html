About us

A Somerset family firm, on site since 1980

K & J Hodge Contractors has been working out of Hammers Yard at Thornfalcon since 1980. Four decades on we are still family run, and still doing the same thing: the groundworks and civil engineering that everything else on a development sits on.

We specialise in residential development, taking on contracts from £200,000 up to £10 million for national housebuilders and construction companies right across the South West. Our own plant, our own operatives and our own drivers means the programme stays ours to keep.

If it needs digging, draining, levelling or surfacing anywhere in Somerset, Devon or North Cornwall, we can price it.
