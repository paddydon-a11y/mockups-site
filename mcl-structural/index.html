About MCL Structural

Below ground is all we do

Headings, pins, piles and basements, on London sites where the neighbours are a metre away and the spoil comes out by the skip. We sheet or timber the ground, dig in sequence, keep everything held as we go and leave a clean formation for the next trade.

How a heading goes in

One lift at a time

1

Look & price

We visit, read the engineer's drawings and price it.

2

Sheet & set

Sheet piles in, first timber frame set at ground level.

3

Dig & frame

Dig a lift, set the next frame, repeat to depth.

4

Pour & clear

Concrete in, spoil away, site handed back clean.
