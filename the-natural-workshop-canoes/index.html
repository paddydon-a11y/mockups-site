Why handmade

We don't compete on price. We compete on pride.

Every canoe leaves our Cambridgeshire workshop shaped by hand and eye, not popped from a mould. They are built for people who care about the details: built to last, built to be admired, and built to actually be paddled.

  • Hand-selected marine ply or hardwood
  • Fully stitched, reinforced construction
  • Painted or varnished inside and out
  • Seats, gunnels, decks and finishing included

How to order

Four steps from idea to the water

1

Choose a design

Pick a hull from the Selway Fisher catalogue. We will help you choose the right one for how and where you will paddle.

2

Tell us the spec

Size, use, timber and finish. Custom paintwork, varnished interiors and camouflage are all on the table.

3

Pay a 50% deposit

Your slot is reserved and the build starts. We only take on a limited number of canoes each season.

4

Collect in 6 to 8 weeks

Pick up your finished canoe from our March workshop. We are happy to help with loading.
