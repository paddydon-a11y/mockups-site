About Taylor Trees

Taylor Trees was founded by Robert in 1990 and is still family run. Gary joined in 2008 after an HNC in Arboriculture and Urban Forestry, and Paul joined in 2014. We work for homeowners, estates, councils and contractors, and our team includes personnel qualified in the detection of bats and other protected species.
