About East Anglian Mechanical

East Anglian Mechanical Limited was set up in 2025 by director Bobby Neal. Bobby brings 22 years of industry experience across new-build projects, retrofit schemes and domestic installations. The company provides consultation, system design and installation, with a focus on energy efficiency and sustainable heating.
