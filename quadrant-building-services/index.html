About Quadrant Building Services

Our delivery team has extensive experience of engineering services in critical environments, with a focus on zero risk to a client's business-as-usual operations. Our core business is supporting clients with extra works and projects, through a specialist fabric and fit-out division and a technical mechanical and electrical division. Regardless of value, every enquiry receives the same attention, planning and supervision.
