About D Benbow Brickwork

Forty years of laying it level

D Benbow Brickwork Ltd is a Sheffield bricklaying firm with more than forty years of experience and knowledge on the trowel. We lay facing brick and blockwork for homeowners, builders and developers: extensions, new build housing, porches, garden walls and repairs. Every job is set out, kept level and finished tidy, from the first course to the last.
