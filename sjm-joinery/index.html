Staircase renovation services

Oak and glass balustrades

Toughened glass panels under a solid oak handrail, in place of spindles.

Spindles and handrails

Worn or dated spindles and handrails replaced with new timber sections.

Oak tread cladding

Existing treads and risers overlaid in oak, without removing the staircase.

Newel posts and caps

New newel posts and caps to match the handrail, in square or turned profiles.

Metal spindle upgrades

Black metal spindles set into an oak handrail and base rail.

Painted staircases

Turned balusters, strings and newels prepared and finished in a painted system.

How a staircase renovation works

1

Photos and measurements

Send photographs and rough sizes of your staircase by WhatsApp or the enquiry form.

2

Design and quotation

We agree the timber, balustrade and finish with you and confirm a written price.

3

Made to measure

Handrails, newels, glass and cladding are prepared to the sizes of your staircase.

4

Fitted on site

The renovation is fitted in your home and the staircase stays in use throughout.

About SJM Joinery

SJM Joinery is a joinery firm based in the North West that specialises in staircase renovation. We keep the structure of your existing staircase and replace what is seen and used every day: the handrail, balustrade, newel posts, treads and risers.

We work in homes across the UK, so a renovated staircase is available wherever you live.
