Our services

We can work directly for main contractors or as part of the M&E or tiling packages.

Design and specification

CAD drawings of the floor build-up and waterproofing before work starts on site.

Wetrooms on timber floors

Modular wet floor system with tapered boards, one or two way falls.

Wetrooms on solid floors

Linear screed drain only 55mm high, with a waterless trap that will not dry out.

Floor and wall waterproofing

Tilesure membrane: waterproof, decoupling and ready to tile, on floors and walls.

Fast track bespoke drains

Wall to wall drains up to 2000mm, made in 2 to 3 days.

After care and guarantee

Site inspections, flood testing and a 10 year installation guarantee on every wetroom.

How we deliver a project

Design

Our design team advises on the floor build-up and, where required, produces CAD drawings for your specific project.

Manufacture

Drains, grills and wet floor systems are made by our sister company CCL Wetrooms in Hook, including bespoke lengths.

Installation

Our operations team liaises with all relevant personnel and trades on site so the programme runs smoothly.

Guarantee

Inspection and flood testing on completion, followed by a 10 year installation guarantee and our After Care Service.
