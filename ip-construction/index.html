About

A Rugby building firm with 30 years behind it

IP Construction (Rugby) Ltd has been building across Rugby and Warwickshire since 2003. Extensions, loft conversions, full new builds and the landscaping to finish them, taken on at any size. Every member of the team holds a CSCS card and completes health, safety and hazardous materials training.
