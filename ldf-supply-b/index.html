Fire safety and compliance

Our fire-rated doorsets are third-party certified by IFC Certification, now part of Kiwa, under its SDP 01 fire door scheme. Independent certification means each doorset design is backed by fire test evidence and audited production, and the paperwork is supplied for your O&M manuals and building records.

FD30 to FD120Fire resistance from 30 to 120 minutes, with S-rated smoke control variants for protected escape routes.
BS 476-22 and EN 1634-130 and 60 minute doorsets certified to both British and European standards, ahead of the 2029 transition.
Complete assembliesCore, frame, seals, glazing and hardware selected together so the doorset performs as tested.
Handover documentsCertification and product documentation supplied with every package.
