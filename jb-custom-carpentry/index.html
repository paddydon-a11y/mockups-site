About J.B Custom Carpentry

J.B Custom Carpentry takes on carpentry and joinery for homes, from a single run of alcove shelving to a full set of fitted wardrobes. Every piece is measured on site, cut to suit the room and fitted by hand, with skirting, architrave and trims finished to match.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Every job starts with a free site visit to measure up, followed by a written, itemised quote before any timber is ordered.

Can fitted wardrobes go into alcoves or under eaves?

Yes. Every wardrobe is made to the measurements of the room, so alcoves, sloping ceilings and uneven walls are planned in from the start.

Which timbers and finishes can I choose from?

Timber, door style, finish and handles are agreed at the site visit, from solid and engineered oak to painted doors, to suit the room.

How long does a carpentry job take?

It depends on the size of the job. The timescale is set out in the written quote, so you know the fitting dates before work starts.

Is the room left clean after fitting?

Yes. Offcuts and dust are cleared at the end of every day and the room is left ready to use when the job is finished.
