Our services

Civils and external networks

Groundworks, ducting, access infrastructure and reinstatement.

  • Duct routes, chambers and external infrastructure
  • Trenching, excavation and reinstatement
  • PIA, new network build and blockage repair

Network infrastructure

The pathways and physical infrastructure for connected environments.

  • External network routes and cabinets
  • Duct and chamber infrastructure
  • Site-specific construction and installation

Telecoms and fibre

Fibre distribution, termination and specialist network installations.

  • FTTH and MDU network installation
  • Fibre termination, splicing and testing
  • Blown fibre, subduct and customer connections

Structured cabling

Copper and fibre infrastructure within buildings.

  • Copper and fibre structured cabling
  • Cabinets, patch panels and cable management
  • Building network installations

Building connectivity

Reliable cabling routes, containment and internal infrastructure.

  • MDU and commercial building cabling
  • Cable containment and distribution
  • Coordinated installation in occupied premises
Certified installer

Passive fire protection

Fire stopping and compartmentation where networks pass through the building fabric.

  • FIRAS certified by Warringtonfire
  • Penetration sealing for service and cable routes
  • Installed alongside our cabling and containment
