About Uisge Water

Uisge Water is a Scottish water hygiene company with a direct, person-to-person service. Our in-house technicians carry out the assessments, sampling and cleaning themselves, and samples are analysed by UKAS-accredited laboratories. That keeps quality consistent and pricing competitive, from Hebridean distilleries to Central Belt hospitals.

Four Steps to Compliance

Site inspection

A full on-site assessment of your water systems, identifying hazards and compliance gaps.

Sampling and analysis

Samples collected and sent to a UKAS-accredited laboratory, or a UKAS lab of your choice.

Reporting and recommendations

Clear findings, recommended actions and a complete logbook for your records.

Ongoing compliance

A monitoring schedule set up for your site, with continuing support.
