How we do it

The method, in detail

Breakout & reinstatement

Small areas
Electric and pneumatic breakers to cut out defective concrete cleanly.
Large volumes
Hydro-demolition, removing concrete without damaging the reinforcement.
Materials
Repair mortars selected per structure for a cost-effective, durable repair.
Labour
Our own skilled, trained operatives on every repair.

Dry spray concrete

Process
Mortar mixed with water and accelerator at the nozzle, sprayed at high velocity.
Why dry
High early strength, flexible on site and minimal waste.
Used for
Strengthening tunnels, bridges and buildings; overlaying cathodic protection.
Finishes
Shot finish, cut and flash, wood or plastic float, steel float.

Structural strengthening

Steel replacement
Damaged steel columns and beams replaced, permanent or temporary.
Steel plate
Mild steel plates, 4mm minimum, epoxy bonded to raise flexural strength.
Carbon fibre wrap
Single or multiple resin-impregnated layers on beams, columns, slabs, walls.
Carbon plate
High-strength plates bonded to soffits, floor slabs and bridge decks.

Protective coatings

Preparation
Dustless blasting, grit blasting and high-pressure washing to a sound surface.
Protects against
Carbon dioxide, water and chloride ingress.
Systems
Anti-carbonation, migratory corrosion inhibitors, cementitious, epoxy, elastomeric.
Result
Longer structure life and fewer costly, disruptive repairs later.

Resin injection & grouting

Fine cracks
Cracks over 0.2mm injected with low-viscosity resin.
Wider cracks
Thixotropic resin, injected under controlled pressure.
Water ingress
Fast-acting hydrophobic resin for a waterproof seal.
Grouting
Pumped cementitious grout to fill voids under structural elements and machinery.
