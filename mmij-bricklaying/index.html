About MMIJ

Bricklayers first, last and always

MMIJ Bricklaying Ltd is a West Yorkshire brickwork firm working for builders, developers and homeowners. Whether it is a full new build package, an extension that has to match the existing house, or a garden wall that has to hold its line for decades, the job gets set out right and laid right.
