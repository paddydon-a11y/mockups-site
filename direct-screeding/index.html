About Direct Screeding

Direct Screeding Ltd was set up by Vaidas in 2016 and works with main contractors, housebuilders and homeowners. The team installs the whole floor build-up in sequence: DPM or radon barrier, PIR insulation, separation layer, underfloor heating and screed, on a labour only or supply and fit basis.
