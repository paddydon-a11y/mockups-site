Marine Scaffolding Services

With extensive knowledge and experience of the marine sector, E&L Scaffolding provides reliable, high quality scaffolding for shipbuilding and for the offshore and onshore energy industries.

We take on scaffolding for both offshore and onshore marine projects, and plan each one around the different access requirements of these environments.

We understand the complex environment of marine scaffolding and work to the highest standards, with every scaffold surveyed, risk assessed and signed off.

Marine Scaffolding Examples

  • Scaffolding for shipyards
  • Scaffolding on wet and dry docks
  • Scaffolding for structures on water, such as docks and piers
