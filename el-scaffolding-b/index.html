About E&L Scaffolding

E&L Scaffolding Ltd is a Liverpool scaffolding contractor with over 17 years of experience, working from our head office on Love Lane. Every job starts with a free site survey and a risk assessment, and every scaffold is put up by CISRS registered scaffolders, signed off and fully insured.

Health and Safety on Every Scaffold

Falls from height are one of the biggest causes of fatal and serious injuries in British construction. A scaffold is only as safe as the people who plan, build and inspect it, so the same checks run through every job, whether it is a single house or a ship in dry dock.

CHAS accredited. CHAS assesses a contractor's health and safety policies and records against recognised standards, so main contractors, councils and schools can see the checks have been passed.

CISRS registered scaffolders. CISRS is the construction industry's scaffolder training scheme. A CISRS card shows a scaffolder has been trained and assessed for the work they carry out.
