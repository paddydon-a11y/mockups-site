Two brothers, one van, the whole list

Hilton's Maintenance is Mackenzie and his brother, working out of St Helens. Most houses have a list of jobs that never gets shorter: the door that sticks, the room that needs painting, the grout that has gone. We come out once and clear it, priced up front.

We keep off the gas, plumbing and electrics, and we do not build extensions. Everything else around the house is ours. You will find us on Checkatrade and Facebook too.
