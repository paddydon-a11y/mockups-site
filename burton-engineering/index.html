Burton Engineering is a full-service engineering and facilities management company providing staff, resources and services to help clients achieve their manufacturing and production goals. We provide maintenance and shutdown cover for the food industry, deliver full turnkey projects and run in-house fibre laser cutting, all from the Innovation Centre on Europarc, Grimsby.

CNC Fibre Laser Cutting

From one-off prototypes to high-volume production, our in-house 12kW fibre laser cuts mild steel up to 40mm thick. We hold a large stock of steel, so many orders are turned round the same day, with next day service available.
