About Baker & Martin Contracts

Baker & Martin Contracts Ltd is a Scottish brickwork subcontractor delivering brick and block packages for main contractors, developers and local authorities. We have an established working relationship with Cruden Group, with packages completed across multiple housing and apartment developments, including foundation brickwork, facing brick, balcony piers and internal stair cores.
