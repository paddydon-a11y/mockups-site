How it works

Three moves. No excuses.

Most outbound fails because it is quiet, polite and part-time. We do the opposite. Volume, persistence and a real conversation with the person who signs.

Named accounts, target titles, the offer. We pressure-test the pitch on day one so every dial after that is sharper than the last.

02 / DIAL

We dial. Relentlessly.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Which sectors do you call into?

All of them. Software, finance, logistics, construction, recruitment, manufacturing, healthcare, energy and professional services, so far. The script changes. The discipline does not.

Do we need to supply the list?

Ideally yes: your named accounts and target titles. If you do not have one we will help you build it before the first dial.

How fast can you start?

Days, not months. Once we have your list, offer and calendar access, we pressure-test the pitch and start dialling.

How do we know what happened on every call?

Every dial is logged with its outcome, duration and next step. You get a weekly report and a live view of booked meetings.

What does it cost?

It depends on volume and how many callers you need. Book a call, tell us the target, and we will give you a number the same day.
