Social housing case studies

General ConstructionSocial Housing

Occupied Block Refurbishment

Overview

External and internal refurbishment of a residential block that remained fully occupied during the works.

Scope of works

Scaffold and protection, envelope repairs, window and communal area works, and coordination of specialist subcontractors.

Challenges and solutions

Works were phased floor by floor, with safe routes kept clear for residents and noisy work held to agreed hours.

Outcome

The block was handed back in stages, with communal areas open to residents at every stage.

Fire and SafetySocial Housing

Fire Safety Remediation Programme

Overview

A programme of fire safety works across the communal areas of a housing provider's residential blocks.

Scope of works

Fire door replacement, fire stopping to service penetrations, compartmentation surveys and cavity barrier repairs.

Challenges and solutions

Work took place in live stairwells and corridors, so escape routes stayed open and doors were changed one at a time.

Outcome

Each block was completed with a full record of the works for the building's fire safety file.

SubcontractingSocial Housing

New Homes for a Housing Provider

Overview

Construction of new affordable homes, delivered as a subcontract package for a main contractor.

Scope of works

Substructure, blockwork and brickwork to the plots, with scaffold coordination and phased handover.

Challenges and solutions

A tight programme on a shared site, managed through weekly look-ahead planning with the main contractor.

Outcome

Plots were completed in phases to suit the main contractor's handover dates.
