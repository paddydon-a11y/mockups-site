About Vartolomei

Design first. Then build.

Vartolomei is a garden design and landscaping company based in Wolverhampton. Every job starts with a design visit: we walk the garden with you, talk through how you want to use it, and agree a plan before any work begins.

Then the same team builds it, from groundworks to the last plant, anywhere in the West Midlands.
