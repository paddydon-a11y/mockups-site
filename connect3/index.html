About Connect3

Connect3 delivers gas, water and electric infrastructure for residential, commercial and industrial developments through one coordinated team, from initial engagement and detailed design through to construction and final connection. The business is led by Managing Director Tom Humphreys, who brings more than 15 years of experience in the utilities industry.

Our Delivery Process

1

Feasibility

Route surveys, trial holes and GPR, with early local authority engagement.

2

Design

Point of connection secured with the network operator and a fully costed design.

3

Construction

In-house civils and network installation, coordinated with your build programme.

Commissioning

5

Adoption

Completed assets adopted by an IDNO, NAV or the incumbent utility.
