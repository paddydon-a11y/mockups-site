How we lay a patio

Step 1 of 4

Dig out

We strip the turf and dig the area out to depth with our own digger, then take the spoil away.

Step 2 of 4

Build the sub-base

A compacted stone sub-base goes down in layers, so the patio stays level and does not sink.

Step 3 of 4

Bed and lay

Each slab is set on a full mortar bed and checked for level and fall, so rainwater runs off.

Step 4 of 4

Point and finish

Joints pointed, edges tidied, and the site swept and cleared before we leave.
