About Revolution Engineering

Revolution Engineering Group is an industrial engineering contractor based in Grimsby, North East Lincolnshire. We fabricate, install and maintain process pipework, tanks, ductwork and steelwork, and carry out welding repairs and planned shutdowns for biomass power, petrochemical and food and beverage sites. Our management systems are certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001.

  • ISO 9001Quality management
  • ISO 14001Environmental management
