About BW Controls

BW Controls began in Bridgwater in 1987, building control panels for factories, quarries and manufacturers. Now led by owner Tom Parsons, we are an independently owned business with a directly employed labour force for panel and electrical installations, working for regional water companies and leading manufacturers.
