About Safari Property Services

Safari Property Services is a local building company specialising in home extensions, renovations, refurbishments and residential improvement projects across Bournemouth, Christchurch, Poole and Dorset.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the property, plan the work carefully, communicate clearly and deliver a high-quality finish. The team brings over 25 years of experience in building and property services.

How we manage your project

Initial Discussion

We discuss your ideas, property, drawings, intended scope and likely timescale.

Site Review and Quotation

We review the available information, visit the property where appropriate and prepare a written quotation explaining the scope, allowances and exclusions.

Planning and Preparation

Before commencing, we agree the programme, access arrangements, key decisions and any information still required.

Construction and Handover

The work is coordinated through the relevant stages, with progress communication and a final review before handover.
