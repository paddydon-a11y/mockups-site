About C&N Scaffolding

C&N Scaffolding & Building Specialists puts up scaffolding for homeowners, builders and contractors from the North West across to the North East. Because we take on building work as well, we know what a scaffold needs to do for the trades working from it, and we plan every job around that from the first visit.
