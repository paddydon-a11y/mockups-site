Kerbing, Paving and Highway Services

Kerbing

Vehicle crossings, channel, edge restraint and decorative kerbs, set to line and level.

Block Paving

Car parks, commercial and retail areas, driveways and patios, in pattern and colour.

Section 278 Works

Junction improvements, crossings, refuges and traffic calming to local authority standards.

Highway and Street Works

Kerb, channel, footway and drainage works on live roads and motorway schemes.

Footpath Construction

Footpaths, cycle paths, shared routes, access ramps and tactile paving to DDA standards.

Surfacing

Tarmacadam and asphalt, resin bound and bonded systems, car parks and lining.

Concreting

Foundations, slabs and bases, stamped finishes and industrial floors to specification.

Groundworks

Excavation, sub-base and formation prepared ready for paving, kerbs and slabs.

Ducting Installation

Service ducts laid, bedded and surrounded ahead of kerbing and surfacing.

Deep Drainage

Pipework, chambers and precast units laid to falls on highway and site schemes.
