Questions

Straight answers on cost and timing

How much does an electrician cost?

A socket installation starts from £80. A full rewire for a 3-bed house costs £3,000 to £5,000. Prices depend on the scope of work, materials and access. We always provide a free, no obligation quote before starting.

What is an EICR and do I need one?

An EICR, or Electrical Installation Condition Report, is a safety check of your wiring and electrics. Landlords must have one every 5 years by law. Homeowners should get one every 10 years, or when buying or selling a property.

How long does a full house rewire take?

A typical 3-bed house rewire takes 5 to 7 working days. That covers stripping the old wiring, installing new cables, fitting a modern consumer unit and testing. We work room by room and restore power at the end of each day, so most families stay in the house throughout.

Do you install EV chargers at home?

Yes, across Yorkshire, Manchester and Liverpool. We fit all the major brands including Pod Point, Zappi and Wallbox. Installation takes 3 to 4 hours and we handle the paperwork, certification and OZEV grant applications.

How do I know if I need a consumer unit upgrade?

If you have a plastic consumer unit, rewirable fuses or no RCD protection, you should upgrade. Properties over 20 years old almost certainly need one. Most upgrades are done in a single day with power back on the same evening.

What should I do in an electrical emergency?

Turn off the main switch on your consumer unit if it is safe to do so, then call us straight away. We cover 24/7 emergency call-outs across Yorkshire, Manchester and Liverpool and aim to be with you inside the hour.

How much do solar panels cost to install?

A typical 4kW system for a 3-bed house costs £5,000 to £7,000. The exact price depends on panel count, roof type and whether you add battery storage. Most systems pay for themselves in 6 to 8 years through bill savings and Smart Export Guarantee payments.

Why should I choose a City & Guilds electrician?

City & Guilds is the most recognised electrical qualification in the UK. It means the electrician has passed assessments on safety, regulations and competence. Delta Electrical is City & Guilds approved with 39 years of experience and full insurance.
