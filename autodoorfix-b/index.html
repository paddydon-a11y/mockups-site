About AutoDoorFix

AutoDoorFix designs, installs and maintains automatic door systems for commercial properties from its base in Wrexham. With close links to the major automatic door manufacturers, the team repairs sound equipment rather than replacing it, and works swiftly and cleanly in busy public areas to keep disruption to a minimum.

Safety, Compliance and Accreditations

Automatic doors in public areas must be safe to use, accessible and kept in good working order. Installations, servicing and inspections are carried out with strict attention to the relevant British Standards and accessibility requirements.

BS EN 16005BS 7036DDA access
ADSAThe Automatic Door Suppliers Association, the UK trade body for powered pedestrian doors and their safe installation and servicing.
GQA QualificationsThe awarding body for glazing, fenestration and door installation qualifications held across the industry.
DBS CheckedDisclosure and Barring Service checks for work in schools, hospitals and care homes.
PASMAIndustry training for the safe use of mobile access towers when working at height.
