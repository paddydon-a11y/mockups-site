About Harveys

Honest advice, tidy work, fair prices

Harveys Tree Services is a Leicester-based tree surgery and landscaping team working across Nottinghamshire, Derbyshire and Leicestershire. Our tree surgeons are NPTC trained and hand picked. We listen to what you want, balance it against the health of the tree and the rules, and recommend the right job, not the biggest one. Materials arrive with us and all waste leaves with us.
