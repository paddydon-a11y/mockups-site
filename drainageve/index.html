Sectors we work in

Industrial and commercial

StormTech® systems carry heavy vehicle loads under service yards, car parks and high-traffic hardstanding, while providing reliable attenuation and infiltration across large impervious areas.

  • Built-in pollution control with the Isolator Row Plus
  • Modular layouts for constrained or phased sites
  • Fully compliant with SuDS regulations

Residential and housing developments

Efficient stormwater management within a compact underground footprint, keeping land free for homes, green space and community areas.

  • Isolator Row pretreatment captures sediment and debris
  • Integrates with permeable paving and other SuDS features
  • Durable, low-maintenance systems that protect local watercourses

Why StormTech® chambers

StormTech® chambers are produced to ASTM standards and designed to meet AASHTO requirements for live load and earth load. They save valuable land and protect water resources under car parks, roadways and heavy earth loads, with more than 30,000 systems in service worldwide.

F900

Strength

Designed for loading up to F900 and proven under HGV service yards and airports. Site vehicles can run over the tank during construction with minimal cover.

80%+

Isolator Row water quality

The patented Isolator Row removes over 80% of total suspended solids, along with metals, hydrocarbons, phosphorus and nitrogen.

No welding

Faster installation

Interlocking chambers and an overlap-only liner mean deep excavations stay open for shorter periods, saving time and cost elsewhere on the project.

Low carbon

Sustainability

Low embodied carbon per cubic metre of attenuated water, and surface space kept free for parking, playgrounds and amenities.
