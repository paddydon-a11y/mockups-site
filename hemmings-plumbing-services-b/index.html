About Hemmings Plumbing

Hemmings Plumbing Services is a Crosby plumbing and heating firm run by Luke. Work covers radiators and heating, bathrooms, repairs and new installations across Merseyside, Ormskirk and Chester. A recent job: a full set of cast iron column radiators for a period house, with new pipe runs laid under the floorboards and each radiator hung on traditional valves.

  1. 01

    Visit and quote

    Luke looks at the rooms, the pipework and the radiators you want.

  2. 02

    Pipework

    New runs laid under the floorboards to each radiator position.

  3. 03
