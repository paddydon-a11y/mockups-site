About Fast Steelfixing

Fast Steelfixing is a labour-only steel fixing and RC frame contractor based in Manchester. Our leading fixers work with site teams, architects and civil engineers to fix reinforcement to the drawings, on programme and in line with site health and safety rules.

Occupied and fully operational sites are never a problem, and we plan, manage and fix multi-phase jobs.
