Zero Waste Cleaning

LM-Cleaning designs avoidable waste out of the contract. Concentrates are refilled on site, bottles stay in service, cloths and mop pads are washed and reused, and steam is used where suitable. The standard of clean stays the same; the bin store sees far less single-use plastic.

  • Refillable concentrates
  • Reusable bottles
  • Washable cloths and pads
  • Steam where suitable
