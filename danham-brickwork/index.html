About DanHam Brickwork

DanHam Brickwork is a bricklaying and building firm working across St Helens and Warrington. Brickwork is where every job starts, but the work goes well past it: complete extensions, two storey builds and full house refurbishments, run as one job from the first course to the finished house. Our motto sums it up: do it right, do it once.
