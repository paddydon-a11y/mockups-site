About T.P Tree Services

T.P Tree Services is a family-run tree surgery and grounds maintenance business based in St Columb, near Newquay. Tom and the team hold NPTC tickets for the work they carry out, are CHAS accredited and carry public and employers' liability insurance, with certificates available on request. Health and safety comes first on every job, from a single garden tree to a roadside contract.
