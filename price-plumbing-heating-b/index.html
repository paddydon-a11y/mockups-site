We know that a new boiler is a significant investment. That is why Price Plumbing and Heating focuses on precision and transparency from start to finish, from the first survey in your home to the demonstration of your new controls.

Our engineers are Gas Safe registered, and every installation meets the highest safety standards. Whether you need a space-saving combi, a powerful system boiler or a traditional regular boiler, we help you choose the right fit for your property.

We treat your home with respect, using floor protection and a thorough clean up once the job is done. Most installations are completed within one to two days, and modern boilers can be up to 30% more efficient than older models.

Based in north Liverpool, the team is run by Jake and works for homeowners, landlords and commercial clients, with support available around the clock for heating emergencies.
