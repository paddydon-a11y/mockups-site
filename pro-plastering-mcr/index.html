What we do

Skimming, boarding and render

Skimming

Full house re-skims, single rooms and ceilings, left flat and clean.

Rendering

Silicone render, sand and cement, base coat and mesh, remedial work.

Insulating

Walls and roofs insulated before the boards go on.

Plaster boarding

Dot & dabbing

Ceilings & coving

About Pro Plastering MCR

From one room to a whole development

Pro Plastering MCR is a plastering and rendering company based in Middleton, working across Greater Manchester and the surrounding areas. With over 25 years of experience, the team takes on everything from small domestic jobs to large commercial developments, including student accommodation and shop renovations.
