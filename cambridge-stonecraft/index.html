What we do

Cut, cleaned & carved by hand

I

Repointing

Lime and cement pointing raked out and made good, matched to the original mortar and colour.

II

Brick & Stone Cleaning

Soot, lichen and staining lifted gently from the face, without damaging the stone beneath.

III

Bay Windows & Cills

New and replacement stone cills, mullions and bay stonework, cut to the original profile.

IV

Columns & Balusters

Turned and carved columns, balustrades and piers restored or renewed in matching stone.

V

Fireplaces

Stone and brick fireplaces built, repaired and restored to their period detail.

VI

Steps

Worn stone steps re-dressed, replaced and laid true, from doorways to churchyard flights.

VII

Carving & Ornament

Hand-carved tracery, finials and mouldings, cut fresh or copied faithfully from the old.

VIII

Restoration & Conservation

Full, sympathetic restoration of listed buildings and churches, from a single stone to a whole elevation.

The people at the chisel

Four decades of working stone

Cambridge Stonecraft has worked the stone of the East of England for over forty years. From the pinnacles of Ely Cathedral to village church windows and the fireplaces of private homes, the team cuts, cleans, repoints and carves by hand. Reliable, honest and highly skilled, they treat every building, listed or lived-in, with the same care.
