About the Register

The Drainage Engineer Register is a centralised platform for maintaining standards across the drainage sector. Engineers upload their qualifications into a secure record. Companies apply for accreditation by meeting industry benchmarks, proving they employ registered engineers, and submitting to regular audit.

The result is one place to check whether a qualification is real, whether it is still in date, and whether the firm holding the contract is who they say they are.

Accreditation framework

Six levels, from first day on site to consultant

0

New starter

Basic induction, visible on the register as working towards Level 1.

1

Technician

Core certifications in place, working under supervision.

2

Engineer

Diagnostics and repairs carried out unsupervised.

3

Senior engineer

Complex problem solving and responsibility for others on site.

4

Specialist

Rehabilitation, CIPP lining and patching, advanced surveying.

5

Consultant

Senior diagnostics, scheme design and expert reporting.

Entry level credentials stay valid for three years unless you progress earlier. Online applications, a searchable national registry and career progression tools are being added to the platform.
