    Compliance and accreditations

    BM TRADA Q-Mark

    Our fire stopping installation, fire door installation and fire door maintenance are certificated under the UKAS accredited BM TRADA Q-Mark scheme. Our workmanship and records are independently audited through regular surveillance visits.

    FDIS Diploma in Fire Doors

    Our Managing Director holds the Fire Door Inspection Scheme Diploma in Fire Doors (DipFD), the recognised qualification for inspecting and specifying fire door assemblies.

    Fire Industry Association

    Members of the FIA, the UK trade association for the fire protection industry, keeping our team current on standards and guidance.

    OneTrace digital reporting

    Every installation is recorded in OneTrace with photographs, locations on the drawings and product details. You receive a complete audit trail at handover, ready for building control, the golden thread and future inspections.

    Constructionline Gold

    Gold Member of Constructionline, so our company, insurance and health and safety information is already verified for main contractor pre-qualification.
