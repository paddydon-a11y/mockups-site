About

One trowel, one standard.

Sam Klimiuk runs S&R Plastering out of Manchester, working across Greater Manchester, Merseyside, Lancashire, Cheshire and West Yorkshire.

Skimming, boarding, ceilings, damp membrane and media walls, taken from bare brick or old artex to a flat finish. Everything gets sheeted down before a bag is mixed, and the job is swept out before he leaves.
