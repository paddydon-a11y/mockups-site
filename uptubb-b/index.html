Falls from Height in Construction and Trade Work

Falls from height are the number one cause of death and serious injury in construction.

31workers killed in falls from height in 2025/26
126workers killed at work in Great Britain that year
25of those deaths were in construction

Source: HSE, Work-related fatal injuries in Great Britain, 2025/26 (RIDDOR).

Introducing UpTuBB

We have developed a tool to help you prevent it.

Work safer from ladders, steps, roof ladders and anywhere you are working at height. UpTuBB is a range of safety tools designed to reduce the risk of falls when using access equipment.

  • Designed for ladders, stepladders and roof ladders
  • Developed by people with practical trade experience
  • Patent pending design
Full product details are released at launch. Waitlist members see it first.

Built by Tradespeople, for Tradespeople

UpTuBB was founded by Graham Wright and is being developed by people with practical trade experience. Every tool starts from the situations tradespeople meet at the top of a ladder, a set of steps or a roof ladder, with one aim: making working at height safer without slowing the job down.
