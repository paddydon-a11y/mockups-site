About B&A

B&A Interior Fit-out Solutions delivers commercial interior fit-outs, shopfitting and refurbishment projects for retail shops, supermarkets, offices, banks, pubs and restaurants across the UK. Our growing team covers joinery, dry lining, ceilings and the final shopfit, with every project managed on site to be delivered professionally, safely and on schedule.
