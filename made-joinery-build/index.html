About us

Built in Liverpool. Made to Last.

Made Joinery & Build Ltd is a Liverpool building company with joinery at its core. We take on extensions, structural work and full project builds for homeowners, and because the first fix and second fix are done by our own joiners, the finish is in our hands from the first stud to the last door.
