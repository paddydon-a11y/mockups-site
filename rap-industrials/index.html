How a renewables project runs

  1. Feasibility analysis and forecasting

    Historic usage data and your future plans confirm the right solution.

  2. Site inspection, specification and design

    A full site inspection identifies the best location for equipment.

  3. Preliminary proposal

    Size and layout, operational costs, funding options and return on investment.

  4. Technical survey and formal proposal

    The detailed survey and final proposal for approval.

  5. Project delivery

    Your account management team manages the project with minimum disruption.

Funding options

Direct Investment

For businesses that want full ownership and control of their renewable energy solution.

  • Large energy cost savings from the outset
  • Full ownership on your balance sheet
  • Smart Export Guarantee

Asset Finance

For businesses that want to spread the cost of a renewable energy solution over a number of years.

  • Manageable and predictable finance costs
  • No upfront CAPEX costs
  • Reduced energy costs

Power Purchase Agreement

For businesses that want to switch to a low-cost renewable energy solution at no cost.

  • No CAPEX cost to your business
  • Fixed price low cost renewable energy
  • No maintenance or management costs

Frequently asked questions

We are looking for a specific part, can you help?

If you are struggling to find the parts you want our team is on hand to help. Contact your account manager and they will be able to help you.

What products do you stock?

We are authorised resellers for many of the major industrial, electrical, fluid power and renewables brands. If we don’t already stock the brand you are looking for we may be able to source it.

Do you deliver?

Yes, all our locations have a fleet of delivery vehicles. When you place your order, we will be able to advise you more about delivery times.

Do you have a trade counter?

Yes, we operate trade counters at all our locations. Please contact the office closest to you for opening times.

What funding options are available for renewable energy solutions?

There are a number of funding options for solar power solutions including purchasing the systems outright, asset finance or a Power Purchase Agreement. Our team can explain each of the options and their benefits.
