About

Stone mason first, builder as well

Masonry and Construction Ltd is run by James, a stone mason and general builder working across South Wales. The work runs from a garden wall or a patio through to extensions, new builds and barn conversions, with the stone and brick done by the same hands that dig the footings. Domestic customers, one point of contact, and walls built to last.
