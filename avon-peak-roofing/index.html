What we do

Every part of the roof, start to finish

01

Roof Repairs

Slipped tiles, leaks, storm damage and damp traced and fixed. Emergency call-outs.

 02

New Roofs & Re-roofs

Stripped, felted, battened and finished in slate or tile. Built to last.

 03

Slate & Tile Roofing

Natural slate, Redland and Marley tiles, laid straight with proper ridge and verge detail.

 04

Flat Roofs

GRP fibreglass, EPDM rubber and felt. Extensions, garages and dormers.

 05

Fascias, Soffits & Guttering

Old timber out, uPVC in. White, black or woodgrain, all debris removed.

 06

Leadwork

Flashings, valleys, soakers and aprons dressed properly so they never let water in.

 07

Chimney Work

Repointing, rebuilds, capping and removal. Stacks made safe and weathertight, flashings renewed at the same time so the job is done once.
