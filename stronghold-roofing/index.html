About Stronghold Roofing

Stronghold Roofing is run by William and takes on everything from a single slipped slate to a full re-roof. He comes out to look at the roof, explains what it needs and gives you a clear price for that work. Homeowners and landlords get one point of contact from the first visit to the last tile.
