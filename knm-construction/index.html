Our preferred method

DOFF steam cleaning

  • High temperature steam delivered at low pressure, so the face of the stone is not damaged.
  • Lifts dirt, grime, paint, algae, moss, lichen and graffiti.
  • Water and steam only. No chemicals and no abrasive blasting.
  • Small volumes of water, so delicate and saturated surfaces stay safe.
