About Lockley's

Lockley's Garden Services is run by Danny, with over 25 years in the trade. He works with a small team of his own across Lancaster, Preston and the Fylde Coast, for homeowners and for commercial sites.

Look for the green and white van. Whether it is a one-off tidy up or a garden looked after all year round, Danny prices it fairly and the team leave it clean.
