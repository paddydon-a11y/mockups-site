The build log

Rear extensions, Halesowen. Six weeks, one team.

Dated site photos from this summer's extensions on one Halesowen road. Same team on every stage, no waiting on other trades.

30 JulStage 1

Footings and blockwork

Dug, poured, insulated and the first courses up.

08 AugStage 2

Steel in

Beams and posts set to take the new opening.

14 AugStage 3

Roof structure

Joists on, rooflight framed, ready for the deck.

22 AugStage 4

Flat roof on

Deck, membrane and edge trims, watertight.

05 SepStage 5

First fix and boarding

Our own electrics and plumbing in, then boarded and skimmed.

12 SepStage 6

Rendered, doors in

Render coat on, bifolds fitted, ready to hand over.

This is how every BJS job runs: one team, dated photos at each stage, and you always know where the build is up to.
