SEED - SCA Business Consultancy, Belfast & London (sca-consultancy.com)

NOT A TRADE, and on Patrick's instruction (2026-09-01) NOT built to the trade
mockup playbook. Rebuilt from scratch as a professional services site: a B2B
cost reduction and business consultancy trading since 2004 as Specialist Cost
Auditors. Branded redesign of their own WordPress site.

Palette:   THEIR MARK, BOTH COLOURS. Bright white page + cool stone #f2f4f8
           section ground + SCA NAVY #0f316a, read straight out of the .st0
           class in their own logo SVG, doing full-bleed duty on the hero, the
           results band, the CTA band and the footer. SCA ORANGE #ff6600 (.st1
           in the same file) is the only accent and it is rationed: one phrase
           in each heading, the primary button, the section eyebrows, the
           result figures, the FAQ plus sign. #cf4d00 is the text-safe cut on
           white, #ffa259 the light on navy. NO orange edges, strips, dashes,
           underbars or dividers anywhere. Patrick, 2026-09-01: "I hate the
           orange edges of boxes."
Type:      Schibsted Grotesk 500-700 display, Inter 400-600 body. One display
           face, no third font, no monospace. Scale and weight do the work.
Idea:      RESTRAINT PLUS SCALE, and the numbers ARE the design. This firm sells
           on figures, so the figures are set large and given room: four real
           proof stats sitting under the hero inside the navy, the eleven
           service lines each closing on a real result, and a full-bleed navy
           results band where six real case figures are the largest type on the
           page after the H1. No specimen sheets, no counters, no calculators,
           no invented client schedules. Everything on the page is a number
           they actually publish.
Layout:    FLUID, NOT BREAKPOINT-PATCHED. Gutters, section padding, gaps and
           every type size are clamp() functions, and every multi-column grid
           has explicit, tested column counts rather than auto-fit, so there is
           no width at which a row ends on a single orphaned cell. Checked at
           390, 480, 560, 660, 768, 900, 1024, 1200 and 1440 rather than only
           at the two canonical viewports. The previous build was only ever
           judged at 390 and 1440 and broke badly everywhere in between.
Loader:    YES, but a brand load rather than a trade animation (Patrick asked for
           "a nice landing screen with their logo on", 2026-09-01). White ground,
           their real full-colour round SCA mark fading up with a slight scale,
           and a 2px hairline track beneath it filling left to right in SCA
           orange. Transform and opacity only. 2.0s then a fade out, with
           fail-safe teardowns at 2.6s and a hard removal at 3.2s; content
           renders underneath and is never gated on JS; reduced motion shows the
           finished mark and bar and lifts immediately. Plays on every load,
           because on a mockup the reload is the demo.
Voice:     Their own strapline is the H1. No owner-name CTAs; John McGowan is
           named publicly as MD so he appears in the team block only. Both
           numbers are landlines, so there is no WhatsApp and no floating call
           button anywhere; the sticky nav carries the Belfast number instead.
Sections:  nav (solid white, their real horizontal SVG lockup, five links and
           the Belfast number set in the display face; brand + burger under 940)
           / hero (full-bleed SCA navy, eyebrow, their strapline as the H1 with
           the third sentence in orange, lede, Book a spend review + Call, and
           four real proof stats on hairline rules underneath, still inside the
           navy) / categories (white, the ten real cost reduction
           categories as photo cards with their real sub-lists, three across at
           desktop with the tenth closing the grid full width; SITS DIRECTLY
           UNDER THE HERO so the first photograph lands on the second screen
           rather than four sections down) / what we do (cool stone, the eleven
           consultancy lines as a two column index, each a number, a name, one
           line and a real result tag) / approach (white, the four methodology
           stages on heavy top rules) / results (full-bleed navy,
           six REAL case figures as the largest type on the page) / quotes
           (white, four REAL client quotes off their case studies page,
           hairline-gridded, attributed by role and client scale) / about and
           team (cool stone, their own Who Are We copy beside the heading, then
           the seven directors they name publicly as a hairline grid) / FAQ
           (white, eight real questions and answers) / CTA (navy) / contact
           (white, both real offices with their real numbers and addresses, the
           real email, and the four-field form) / footer (deep navy, white
           lockup, credit to the construction-sites.co.uk root with the naked
           URL anchor).
