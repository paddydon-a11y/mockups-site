Common questions

The things finance directors ask us first

What can SCA actually do for me?
We are procurement efficiency and business consultancy specialists delivering sustainable, measurable savings while highlighting opportunities to increase your profitability. We can help you reduce costs, improve quality and service delivery, strengthen supplier relationships, assess internal processes and meet compliance targets. We will also introduce a few bespoke business solutions that are not readily available on the open market.
Anyone can cut costs by buying cheaper. How do I know you are not just doing that?
You are in control of the process and every decision from start to finish. Our business model is to win your trust through project delivery, and matching proven suppliers to the needs of your business is a core part of how we work. In all cases savings are implemented without a reduction in the quality or service levels you enjoy now.
What can you do that we cannot do ourselves?
We are an extension of what you do now, not an alternative. We have access to prices paid by many other organisations, which you do not, and we use that data to negotiate specifically on your behalf. We also have expertise in the areas of expense that sit far down your priority list, so those get looked at without disrupting your own department.
Must I leave my existing suppliers?
No. Over 60% of our projects end with the client getting better service and lower prices from the suppliers they already have. You remain in control of the decision, only this time you are making it on independent data and impartial advice.
How soon will we see results?
It varies by spend category, but usually within 6 to 12 weeks you will have new purchasing options to act on. Existing supplier contract terms may affect the timing.
Will you work on a no savings, no fees basis?
Yes. Several of our clients prefer to work that way. Fee structures are agreed with you before any project starts, so there are no surprises either way.
What happens after implementation?
We monitor and report on actual savings and service delivery, usually for the next 24 months. Detailed supply audit reports are prepared monthly, quarterly or biannually as you prefer. They check prices, detect refunds and credits due, and demonstrate actual savings, which is useful when you need to prove to colleagues that the numbers are genuine.
We do not have the time or the budget for this.
Our team does the research and the analysis, then works with you to implement only the actions you have approved. You do not need budget or internal resource to engage us. Think of it as extra capacity rather than another project on your list.
