About GM Construction

Three years, and most of it up a corridor.

GM Construction is a painting and decorating outfit based in Nelson, working across Pendle, Burnley and the rest of East Lancashire. A lot of the work is communal blocks and commercial units: masked up, filled, sprayed out and back in use quickly, with the cutting in done by hand where a gun cannot reach.

The rest is houses. Same order of work, same standard, just quieter.
