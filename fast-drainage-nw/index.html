Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to clear a blocked drain?

Most standard blockage clearances start from around £80 to £150 depending on the job. Bigger problems like jetting or camera surveys cost a little more. We give you a clear price before we start.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No, there is no separate call-out fee. You get a clear price for the work and one of our team comes out to do it personally.

Are shared drains handled differently?

Yes. Shared drains are the responsibility of United Utilities. If the blockage sits on a shared section, we let you know straight away and pass it over to them. We clear and fix drains on your own property.

How quickly can a blocked drain be cleared?

Most blockages are cleared in a single visit, often within an hour or two of arriving. We are open 24 hours a day, so urgent jobs are usually reached the same day.
