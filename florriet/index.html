About Florriet

A local team you can actually reach

Florriet Ltd is a small Aylesbury building company, a team of three handling general building, extensions, driveways, patios and the handyman jobs in between. Because it stays small, the person quoting the work is the person doing it, and you deal with one number the whole way through. We keep to what we are good at, so we do not take on electrical or plumbing, and we cover Aylesbury and roughly 30 miles around it.
