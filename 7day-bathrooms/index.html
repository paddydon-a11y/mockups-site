Who We Are

Four decades of getting bathrooms right.

7Day Bathrooms is run by Ian Charles, a bathroom fitter and builder with over 40 years in the industry. Based in Egham, Surrey, Ian and the team specialise in complete bathroom renovations: strip-out, first fix, installation, panelling or tiling, and a spotless handover.
