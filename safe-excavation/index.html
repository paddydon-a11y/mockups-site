About Safe Excavation

Suction excavators, hired out with the operator

Safe Excavation has been hiring out suction excavators since 2016, out of the yard at Medway Freight Centre in Strood. The fleet runs from 32 tonne trucks down to a remote controlled crawler that fits through a side gate. Motorway works, civils, utilities and building sites: the machine is matched to the ground, and every operator comes CPCS trained.
