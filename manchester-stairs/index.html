About us

Two friends, one workshop, since 2018

Manchester Stairs Limited was founded in 2018 by two friends with years in the trade. We manufacture timber staircases from Unit 9B on the Kayley Industrial Estate in Ashton-under-Lyne and deliver across the North West of England.

We are a supply-only workshop. We do not fit, but we can recommend reliable joiners who do. Softwood and hardwood, single flights or bulk orders for builders and housing associations, every one hand finished before it leaves.
