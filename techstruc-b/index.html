About Techstruc

Techstruc is a structural repairs and construction company based in Crosby, Liverpool, with over 30 years in the building industry. We are accredited with the WTIF for cavity wall tie installation and hold CHAS Advanced. We work alongside structural engineers on surveys, lintels, DPC and knock-throughs, and deliver residential, commercial and retrofit projects with our own in-house team.
