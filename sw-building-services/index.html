How We Work

Back to brick, then done once

Day One · Stripped back to brick
Handover · The same flat, weeks later

SW Building Services is a Fulham based design and build team. We strip flats and houses back to brick, sort the structure and services, then bring every room back with joinery, panelling and finishes chosen with you. Most of our work comes by recommendation from homeowners, landlords and letting agents across south west London.
