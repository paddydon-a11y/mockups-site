About A&G Setherton

A&G Setherton is run by Alan and Gary Setherton from Bishops Nympton, near South Molton. Our own builders and stonemasons, backed by subcontractors we have used for many years, work for homeowners, architects and community groups across North Devon. Most of our work comes from recommendation.
