About Ovonis

A specialist water, drainage and leak detection company based in Birmingham. We investigate the problem, explain the findings and any uncertainty, and carry out suitable repairs where the work is within our scope.

Our investigation process

Investigate

The right equipment for the symptoms.

Identify

The defect, leak or blockage and its cause.

Report

Findings recorded, uncertainty stated.

Recommend

Access, maintenance or repair options.

Repair

Suitable repairs, completed work recorded.

Methods include CCTV inspection, acoustic listening, tracer gas, thermal imaging and drainage tracing.
