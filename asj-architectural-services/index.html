How it works

One set of drawings, two approvals

1
Visit, measure and designWe look at the property, measure up and agree the layout with you.

Planning permission

  1. Plans and elevations drawn to show the council what is proposed.
  2. Application submitted with the forms and supporting drawings.
  3. Decision from the council, with any queries answered along the way.

Building regulations

  1. Construction drawings showing structure, insulation, drainage and fire safety.
  2. Submitted to building control for checking.
  3. Approval to build, and a set your builder can price from.
2
Ready to buildApproved drawings in hand, ready for quotes and a start date.
