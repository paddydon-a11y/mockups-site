About Arkway

Twenty five years under the ground

Arkway was founded by Mark Gray, who has spent over 25 years in building and construction. The company runs its own groundworks, masonry and diamond cutting teams out of Silsden, with site managers holding SMSTS and the business accredited through CHAS and SafeContractor.

Work runs from commercial substructure and civils through to stone restoration, extensions and walling for private clients.
