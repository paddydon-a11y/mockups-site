About Roof Makers

Roof Makers is a roofing company working across Surrey and South West London. Jobs range from a single slipped tile to a full strip and re-roof, on pitched and flat roofs alike. Every job is quoted in writing, the site is kept tidy and you get photos of the finished roof.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cover my part of Surrey or South West London?

Roof Makers works across Surrey and South West London, from Kingston, Wimbledon and Richmond out to Epsom, Weybridge, Woking and Guildford. If your town is not listed, call or send a message and we will confirm.

Can you quote for a roof repair from photos?

Often, yes. Send photos of the problem area and the roof as a whole, and we can usually give a guide price before booking a visit.

Do I need a full re-roof or will a repair do?

It depends on the age and condition of the tiles, battens and membrane. After a survey we will say which is needed, and price both where it makes sense.
