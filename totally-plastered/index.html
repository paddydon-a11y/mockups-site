About us

Family run, with roots set in Burnley

Totally Plastered is a tongue in cheek name for a family run Burnley plastering firm. We take a wall from bare brick to a finished skim, and we sort the damp before we cover it up. Our view has always been simple: if you are going to do a job, do it right, because good work leads to good work.
