About LSJ Engineering

Made in Shoeburyness, fitted across the South East

LSJ Engineering has provided steel construction works for businesses and commercial ventures since 2002. We work with engineers and architects from the design stage, and our draughting and detailing is done in house by experienced staff. One friendly team takes your job from first survey to final fit.
