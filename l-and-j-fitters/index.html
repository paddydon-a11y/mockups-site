About L & J Fitters

L & J Fitters Ltd is owned by husband and wife team Leigh and Jessica Goodall. The company began in 2014 with Leigh and four fitters installing kitchens in newly built houses, and now runs more than 40 fitters on contracts across the country.

All of our team are trained and accredited with CSCS and manual handling, and work in full uniform with PPE and the correct tools. Our team leaders are SMSTS or SSSTS trained and qualified first aiders.
