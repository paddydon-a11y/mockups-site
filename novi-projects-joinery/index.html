About us

Qualified joiners who stay to the end

Novi Projects Joinery is a joiner-led team working out of Inverness and across the Highlands. We started in joinery and house maintenance and grew into full extensions, garden rooms and whole house renovations, so one team can take a job from the first strip out to the last coat of paint.

Recent work includes a full house strip out with a new 28m² rear extension built from the ground up, and a 6.2m by 4.4m insulated garden room.
