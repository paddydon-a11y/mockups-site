About

Straight prices from a vehicle buyer, not a forecourt

John Van Sales & Commercials is run by John Ward from Nottingham. John buys cars, vans and commercial vehicles direct from private sellers, tradespeople and businesses, and travels the length of the country to collect them. No forecourt, no middleman, no messing about: send the details, get a price, and if you are happy the vehicle is collected and paid for.
