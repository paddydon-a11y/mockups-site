A Family Business for Over 30 Years

P.M.C.E is a family run utility contractor, previously known as Pat Mulhern Civil Engineering. Pat has passed the majority of the running of the company to his two sons, Patrick and Shane. Our commitment is to exceed client expectations and build lifetime relationships on integrity, quality and performance.

Our Leadership Team

Pat Mulhern Snr

Founder

Pat began his career in the utility industry in 1973, working primarily on major water mains projects before expanding into the gas sector in the 1990s. Following 15 years as a team leader, he established Pat Mulhern Civil Engineering Ltd in 1988, contracting to John Kennedy CE Ltd and undertaking all aspects of water and gas pipeline works. The company was officially renamed PMCE Ltd in 2010.

Pat takes great pride in the quality and efficiency of PMCE’s work. He remains closely involved in site operations, bringing decades of practical experience and a hands-on approach to the business.

Patrick Mulhern

Managing Director

Patrick joined PMCE in 2014 after completing his degree in Business Management, continuing the family’s involvement in the utility industry. Having gained practical experience on site, he progressed to Site Supervisor in 2016, Operations Manager in 2018, and Contract Manager and Director in late 2019, before taking on his current role as Managing Director.

Guided by the Kaizen principle of continuous improvement, Patrick has helped drive the company’s year-on-year growth while building strong, lasting client relationships. He is committed to exceeding client expectations through high standards of health and safety, compliance and customer service, alongside reliable delivery against project targets.

Shane Mulhern

Director

Shane joined the company in 2004 as a trainee gas operative, progressing to team leader and SCO Competent Person by 2006. He has since delivered numerous pipeline installations across the UK, broadening the company’s expertise and overcoming complex challenges to meet clients’ requirements.

With a strong focus on quality and efficiency, Shane brings a practical, customer-focused approach to every project, ensuring work is completed to the highest standard and to the client’s satisfaction.
