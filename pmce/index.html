Our Leadership Team

Pat Mulhern

Managing Director

Pat began in the utility industry in 1973, predominantly working on large water mains, before expanding into the gas sector in the 90s. After 15 years as a team leader he formed Pat Mulhern Civil Engineering Ltd in 1988, as a contractor to John Kennedy CE Ltd, undertaking all aspects of water and gas pipelines. In 2010 the company name was officially changed to PMCE Ltd.

Pat prides himself on the efficiency and quality of pipelines completed by PMCE, and is never far from the frontline.

Shane Mulhern

Director

Shane began working for the company in 2004 as a trainee gas operative, quickly progressing to team leader and SCO competent person by 2006. He has carried out many pipeline installations across the UK, expanding the company's experience and overcoming difficult challenges to make sure each job is completed to the client's satisfaction.

Quality and efficiency of the task at hand, with the customer in mind, is his main focus.

Patrick Mulhern

Director

Following in the footsteps of the company's origins, youngest son Patrick joined PMCE in 2014 after completing his degree in Business Management. After a short stint on site he became Site Supervisor in 2016, Operations Manager in 2018, and Contract Manager and Director in late 2019.

Applying the Kaizen approach of continuous improvement, the company has grown year on year while building strong, long-standing relationships with clients. Patrick's values stand firmly on exceeding client expectations on Health and Safety, Compliance, Customer Service and Delivering targets.
