About Fletcher & Goodwin

Fletcher & Goodwin is a VAT registered plastering and rendering company based in Wigton. Homeowners come first, with commercial work and subcontract plastering for local builders alongside. No job is too big or too small, from patching one ceiling to rendering a whole house. Estimates are free, and every job is left clean.
