About MJA Properties

MJA Properties is a building company based in Cornwall. We take on brickwork, plastering, extensions and refurbishments, and we specialise in listed buildings and heritage work, where older properties need repairs and alterations that respect how they were built. One of our listed building projects has been featured on the BBC.
