About Tattenhall Plumbing & Heating

Chris Wiseman set up Tattenhall Plumbing & Heating in November 2018, after 15 years with a respected local firm. Matthew Rumsey joined the business in 2020. Most of the work is for homeowners across Cheshire and North Wales, with commercial jobs alongside. Quotes are free, with no obligation, and priced to suit the budget.
