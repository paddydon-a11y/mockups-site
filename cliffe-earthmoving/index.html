About Cliffe Earthmoving

Cliffe Earthmoving is a family run company based in Stoke-on-Trent, Staffordshire. We take on all earthmoving and earthworks, from stripping a site to cutting and filling it to level, and we work across the UK. Alongside our own work we supply plant on a self drive hire basis.
