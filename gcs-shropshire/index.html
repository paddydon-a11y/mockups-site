About GCS

A Shropshire outfit with main contractor experience

GCS Shropshire Ltd is a civil engineering subcontractor. We supply operated plant and labour-only gangs to main contractors, developers and local authorities across Shropshire and the counties around it.

Kerbing, tarmac, block paving, slabbing and pipe laying are what we do every day, taken on as a full subcontract package or as gangs working to your programme.
