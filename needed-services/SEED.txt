SEED — Needed Services Ltd, Rugby, Warwickshire
Brief:     Multi-trade construction company, Rugby CV22. Their own strapline on both their
           leaflet and their Wix site is "Build. Supply. Maintenance." and their leaflet
           splits the business into THREE DIVISIONS: Build & Groundworks / Maintenance &
           Cleaning / Decoration & Fit-outs. 12 real job photos: a full garden
           transformation (resin path, porcelain steps, composite decking, painted
           fencing), a kitchen extension with a lantern, decking set-out and finished,
           a cafe fit-out, a pub refurb (exterior, dining room, washrooms), external
           paintwork and prep. No logo file anywhere, no reviews anywhere, no address
           beyond "Rugby, Warwickshire".
Concept:   ONE TEAM, THREE DIVISIONS, RUN AS A SYSTEM. The whole site is driven by their
           own three-division structure: the hero photo, the services and the gallery all
           answer to the same 01/02/03 selector, so a prospect can see the one firm doing
           three different things without ever leaving the page. This is the pitch on
           their own leaflet ("ONE TEAM. MULTIPLE TRADES."), executed as a mechanism
           rather than a headline.
