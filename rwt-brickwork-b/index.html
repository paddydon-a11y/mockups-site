About RWT Brickwork

RWT Brickwork LTD is a brickwork contractor working across Wales. We price brick and blockwork packages from drawings, agree the programme with your site team and build plot by plot to the specification. Rhodri is your direct contact from the first price to the last plot. Smaller projects are still taken on where they fit around site work.
