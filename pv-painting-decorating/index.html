About PV Painting and Decorating

PV Painting and Decorating Services takes on single rooms, whole houses and house fronts. Every job starts with the preparation: furniture covered, floors sheeted, cracks filled and surfaces sanded before any paint goes on. You get a written quote first, a start date that holds, and a clean house when the last coat is dry.
