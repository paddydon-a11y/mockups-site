About G & L Coldstore Construction

G & L Coldstore Construction Ltd is a family run company based in St Helens, Merseyside, focused on coldstore construction and hygiene applications. Every job is planned in detail and built with high quality materials, and we look after coldrooms once they are running. All of our quotes are free.
