About Ziggy Gee Contractors

Ziggy set up the company in April 2022, bringing more than 15 years of experience in site clearance, fencing, landscaping, tree surgery and vegetation management across private and commercial work. That experience covers project, site and contract management as well as the work itself, with a firm grounding in health and safety.
