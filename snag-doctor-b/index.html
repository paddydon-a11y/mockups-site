About Snag Doctor

Our technicians repair chips, dents, scratches and holes on wood, metal, stone, brick, marble, granite and ceramic, on site and in situ. There is no waiting for a replacement and no chain of trades to book in. Clients regularly save between 50 and 70 percent against replacement, and less waste goes to landfill.
