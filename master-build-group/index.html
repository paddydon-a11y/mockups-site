About Master Build Group

A family firm, founded by two brothers

Master Build Group is a family-owned construction and renovation company based in Northampton. It was founded by two brothers, one from an electrical background and one from plumbing and gas engineering, and has grown into a full building firm with a trusted team of trades. Small upgrade or full build, every project gets the same planning and the same care.

  • Up to £5 million public liability insurance
  • Residential and commercial projects
  • One point of contact from survey to handover
