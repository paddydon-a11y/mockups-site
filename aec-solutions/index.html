About AEC

Automation Engineering & Controls was formed in June 2019 by six directors from the automotive and service sectors, to provide affordable automation without compromising on technology or technique. Our engineers come from time served backgrounds and senior management roles, and we work across the automotive, food, construction and defence sectors.
