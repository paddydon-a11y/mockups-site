Treatment plant servicing, before it stops working

Cold weather, heavy rainfall and increased usage all put extra pressure on a sewage treatment plant. A regular service picks up problems before they turn into a costly breakdown.

  • Full inspection of the treatment plant
  • Cleaning and removal of accumulated sludge where required
  • Pump and float checks
  • Air blower and diffuser checks
  • Electrical and control system checks
  • Inlet and outlet inspection
  • Inspection for blockages and restricted flow
  • General condition and operational checks
  • Advice on any repairs or maintenance required
