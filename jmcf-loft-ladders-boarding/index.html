From nowhere to stand to a floor you can walk on.

What we start with

Joists, insulation, nowhere safe to stand

Most lofts are just insulation between the joists. Stand on it and you squash it, and you are one slip from the ceiling below.

What you get back

A boarded floor, a ladder and a light

Boards go on raised timbers so the insulation keeps its depth, then a ladder and a light so the space is genuinely usable.

What we do

Everything that turns a loft into usable space.

01

Loft Boarding

Raised boards over the insulation, laid tight and screwed down.

02

Loft Ladders

Timber or aluminium, set to swing down clear of the landing.

03

Loft Hatches

Small hatches opened out to a size you can climb through.

04

Loft Lighting

LED battens fitted so the whole loft is lit, switch by the hatch.

05

Loft Storage

Walkways and decked bays set out around what you actually store.

06

Loft Improvements

Bigger openings, insulation top ups and general loft upgrades.

About JMCF

Loft work, and nothing else.

JMCF is Ryan, working out of Wigan and covering around 50 miles from home. Lofts are all he does, so the boards go down over the insulation rather than on it, the ladder is set to land clear of the landing, and the light goes in while he is up there. Most lofts are done in a day, and the work comes mostly on recommendation.
