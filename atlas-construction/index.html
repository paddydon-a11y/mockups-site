About Atlas

Atlas Construction was founded by Luke Cooper to offer dependable, high quality building work to homeowners and businesses across Devon. What started as a small, family focused business now takes on everything from single room renovations to new homes and commercial projects, with one team managing the design, building, plumbing and electrical work.
