Who we are

We build the part nobody ever sees.

Eastern Substructures is a substructure bricklaying and beaming outfit working across Cambridgeshire. We take plots from the finished footing up to DPC and hand them over decked and ready. Housebuilders use us because the gauge holds, the cavities stay clean, and plots come off in the order the programme says they will.
