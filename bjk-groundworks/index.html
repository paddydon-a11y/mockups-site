About BJK

Set up to take on the big ones

BJK Groundworks & Plant Hire works out of Armagh and covers the whole of Northern Ireland. Around thirty groundworkers, our own excavators, dumpers and rollers, and the drivers to run them.

That means we can take a groundworks package on from site clearance right through to finished formation, on housing sites and civils contracts as well as one-off jobs for private clients.
