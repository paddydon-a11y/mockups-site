The Studio

Cabinet making precision, at the window

Form & Fenestra is run by Duncan Matthews from a workshop at Southfield Farm, Canon Frome, near Ledbury. Behind it sit City & Guilds cabinet making training and eighteen years building fine furniture in London, including work for Sir Terence Conran and David Linley.

That standard now goes into your shutters: measured, made and fitted by Duncan himself.
