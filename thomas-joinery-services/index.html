Who we are

Carpentry, passed down the bench.

Thomas Joinery Services is a Bedford joinery workshop with over 35 years at the bench, a trade passed down from generation to generation. We design, make and fit bespoke wardrobes, kitchens, staircases and one-off pieces, using materials from names we trust so the work lasts for years.

Every job starts with a free design consultation and ends with a piece built for the room it lives in, not pulled off a shelf.
