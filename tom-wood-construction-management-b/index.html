About Tom Wood Construction

Tom Wood Construction & Management is a Leyburn builder and main contractor working across a very rural patch of North Yorkshire. Each job is run as a complete package, with the same trusted subcontractors brought in time after time, so the stone work, groundworks, building and kitchen work all sit under one contract and one person.
