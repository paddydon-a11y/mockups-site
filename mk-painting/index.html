About Martin

Martin Kingsnorth has run his painting and decorating business since 1998 and has close to three decades in the trade. The work covers private homes, commercial premises and industrial units, inside and out. Every quote is free, every room is protected before work starts, and every job is finished to the standard written on his sign.
