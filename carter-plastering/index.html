About

Run by Josh, backed by his finish

Carter Plastering & Rendering is run by Josh, working out of Manchester on everything from bedroom re-skims to shopping centre fit-outs and full external renders. Builders bring him back job after job, and the reviews all say the same two things: the finish is spot on, and the place is left clean.
