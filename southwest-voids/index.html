About Southwest Voids

Southwest Voids LTD is a growing multi-trade property refurbishment and maintenance contractor working across the South West of England and South Wales. We return void properties to a lettable standard and deliver building works for public and private landlords, supported by certified management systems for quality, environment and health and safety.

Our void turnaround process

1

Keys received

Property inspected and the scope of works agreed with your team.

2

Strip out

Clearance, strip out and the property made safe for works.

3

Multi-trade works

Repairs and refurbishment programmed across the trades.

4

Clean and snag

Inspection, snagging and a full clean before handover.

5

Ready to let

Keys returned with the property at your lettable standard.
