About Predator Marine

Predator Marine Limited is a Lowestoft-based commercial marine operator. Our team brings backgrounds in commercial vessel operations, offshore support, navigation and specialist marine projects. We work with survey companies, offshore contractors and project managers to agree the scope, then supply a suitable vessel, skipper and operating plan, for a single assignment or a long-term contract.
