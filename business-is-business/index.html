About us

Business Is Business

We are a tape and jointing company. We specialise in taping and jointing plasterboard and skimming walls and ceilings, and every job goes through the same four passes: first coat, second coat, final and rubbing down. The decorator walks onto a flat wall with nothing left to fill.

How we work

Four passes to a flat wall

01

First coat

Tape bedded into every joint and the screw heads spotted.

02

Second coat

Joints widened and feathered out well past the tape.

03

Final

A last wide coat so the joint disappears into the board.

04

Rubbing down

Sanded smooth and flat, ready for mist coat and paint.
