A family run father and son business

Onyx Resin Surfaces is Larry Hansford and his son Ryan. Larry has more than 35 years in resin flooring, on jobs including the Millennium Dome, Vauxhall Motors and the 2012 Olympic Athletes Village. Ryan learned the trade on site, specialising in MMA systems and large projects, and now oversees every install. As a father and son team, you get personal accountability and a name you can trust.
