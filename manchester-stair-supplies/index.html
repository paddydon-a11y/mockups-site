Made to order

The whole staircase, made in Ashton

Parts are half the story. Manchester Stairs Limited, our sister company in the same unit, manufactures complete flights to your three measurements. Straight, quarter turn, half turn, paddle and outdoor, designed to the building regulations and hand finished before delivery.

Buying guide: know your parts

Tap a number to see what each part is called and does.

  • 1
    StringThe long side board the treads sit in.
  • 2
    TreadThe part you stand on.
  • 3
    RiserThe upright between treads.
  • 4
    HandrailBetween the newels, or on wall brackets.
  • 5
    Base railGrooved rail the spindles sit into.
  • 6
    SpindleThe uprights. Square, chamfered or turned.
  • 7
    NewelThe post at each end.
  • 8
    Newel capThe finishing piece on the post.
  • 9
    Handrail bracketFixes a wall handrail.
About us

Supplied by the people who make them

Manchester Stair Supplies sells the same parts our workshop uses on its own staircases. Softwood or oak, one spindle or a full landing's worth, for builders, joiners and homeowners across the North West.

We are supply only. We do not fit, but we can recommend reliable joiners who do. Bulk orders for builders and housing associations welcome.
