Who we are

A West Dorset firm that finishes what it starts

Chapman Building & Plastering has been a limited company since 2012, based between Axminster and Bridport and covering West Dorset and East Devon. New builds, extensions, listed buildings and conversions, with the plastering and rendering done in house. Most of what we take on is the whole job: we start at the footings, bring the other trades in ourselves, and you deal with one number until the keys go back.
