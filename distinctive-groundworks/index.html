About us

One of the South East's leading groundworks contractors

Distinctive Groundworks Ltd is a family run business based in Ashford, Kent, with over 45 years experience in the construction industry. We work across the commercial, residential and retail sectors, integrating with the client's team, highlighting risks early and value engineering around on site constraints.

We supply labour, plant and materials with our own engineering and supervision, so we keep total control over the quality of the work, the programme and the cost.
