About YS Cabling Solutions

YS Cabling Solutions is a London cabling subcontractor led by director Ber Sufaj. Our team of electricians, cable jointers and cable pullers installs, joints and terminates HV and LV cables for main contractors, M&E contractors and electrical contractors, from tray and ladder runs to large multicore XLPE cables terminated into switchgear.
