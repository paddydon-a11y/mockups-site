About Stone Cross Projects

Stone Cross Projects Ltd is a commercial refurbishment contractor based in Walton-on-Thames, previously trading as Hugh Forbes Building Company. We take office space from dilapidations and strip out through to a complete fit out, with one team responsible for the programme from the first survey to handover.

Our Process

Survey and Quotation

We visit the space, review the schedule or brief and price the works.

Strip Out

The existing fit out is removed and the space taken back to shell.

Build and Fit Out

Partitions, ceilings, flooring, joinery and finishes installed to programme.

Handover

Snagging completed and the space handed over clean and ready to occupy.
