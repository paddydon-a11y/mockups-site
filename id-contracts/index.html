About I.D Contracts

One kitchen fitter, the whole job covered

I.D Contracts is run by Ian, a kitchen fitter and joiner working out of Stockport. Ian fits the kitchens and does the joinery himself, so the person who quotes the job is the person on site doing it. For the work that needs a ticket, he uses an electrician and qualified plumbing and gas trades, which means you book one job rather than chasing three.
