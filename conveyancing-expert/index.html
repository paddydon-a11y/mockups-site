About the Firm

Property specialists, nothing else

Conveyancing Expert has provided expert legal services to the residential property market since 2009. We started life as a primarily online conveyancing practice, and in 2011 we moved to regulation by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers, a much better home for what we do. Our solicitors remain fully registered on the roll of solicitors and as licensed conveyancers.

Conveyancing, probate and wills are all we handle. No family law, no personal injury, just property specialists. Most of the team have grown with the firm since 2009, and our investment in technology means your case moves quickly, wherever you are in England and Wales.

  • No completion, no fee
  • Price-beat guarantee on comparable quotes
  • One dedicated conveyancer on your case
  • Fully online, wherever you are in the UK
  • Updates at every stage, so you are never chasing
