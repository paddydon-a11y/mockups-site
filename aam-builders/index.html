About

A build team, not a phone list

AAM Builders UK Limited is a Birmingham building company running a team of around 20. Because the trades are ours, a loft, an extension or a full refurbishment does not turn into a hunt for a plumber and an electrician halfway through. We plan the whole build, put our own people on site, and keep one point of contact from the first quote to the final handover. It is what lets us take on the larger residential jobs many one man outfits have to turn away.
