About us

One team, one number, from strip out to handover

Atherton Construction Solutions is a seven-strong building team working out of Manchester, with projects currently running in Derby. We buy and renovate older properties ourselves, so we know what is behind the plaster before we price it. Every job is managed by us from first survey to final snag, with our own electricians on the team.
