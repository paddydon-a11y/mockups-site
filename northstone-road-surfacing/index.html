About Northstone

Northstone Road Surfacing is a UK road surfacing contractor specialising in high-quality asphalt and tarmac surfacing. We provide reliable surfacing solutions for commercial, civil engineering and private projects, from highway resurfacing and car parks to reinstatements and patch repairs. We are based in the North West and work nationwide.

Our Surfacing Process

1

Survey and Quotation

Site visit, measurements and a clear written quotation.

2

Planing and Preparation

Existing surface planed off, edges cut and the base made sound.

3

Base and Binder Course

Regulating and binder layers laid to the correct levels and falls.

4

Surface Course

Asphalt surface course laid by paver and rolled to finish.

5

Joints and Handover

Joints sealed, site cleared and the surface handed back.
