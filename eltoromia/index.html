About Eltoromia

Eltoromia is a landscaping company based in Esher, run by Jamie. The team designs and builds gardens from the ground up: groundwork, drainage, paving, brickwork, turf and fencing, and looks after them afterwards. Customers mention the same things again and again: clear advice at the quote, steady updates on site and a garden left clean and tidy at the end.
