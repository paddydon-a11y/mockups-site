About TL Joinery & Construction

TL Joinery & Construction is a Liverpool joinery and building firm run by Tom. We take on full joinery packages on new builds and renovations, along with extensions and loft conversions, for homeowners and commercial clients across Merseyside and the North West. One team handles the timber work from first fix through to the finished room.
