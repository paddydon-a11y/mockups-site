About BV Construction

One call covers the whole job.

BV Construction is a Richmond building company specialising in loft conversions, house extensions and complete property refurbishments. Because the plumbing and electrical work stays in-house, one team carries your project from first survey to final sign-off. We work with homeowners, landlords and property developers.
