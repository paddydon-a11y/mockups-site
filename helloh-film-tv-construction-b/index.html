About HELLOH

Liam O'Hara started out as a joiner, moved into construction management and has led HELLOH since 1998. He is still hands-on as director. Every production gets its own construction manager, backed by a core team of carpenters, fabricators, plasterers and scenic artists, and a scenic painting department respected across the industry.
