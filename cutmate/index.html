Meet Graham, inventor of CutMate

Graham Waring is a Dorset inventor and DIY enthusiast. CutMate started with a problem he kept meeting while cutting boards: keeping the offcut supported as the cut finishes, so it does not drop, move or splinter. What began at home in Dorset is now a British invention heading into wider production.

How CutMate works

Clip it onto the board

No separate support or clamp to set up first.

Make your cut

Circular saw, handsaw or track saw, on timber, plywood or MDF.

Finish the cut supported

The loose end is held as the cut completes, with less movement.

Board thicknesses and saw compatibility

CutMate is designed for boards of 12mm, 15mm and 18mm. Check your board thickness and the CutMate version before use.
