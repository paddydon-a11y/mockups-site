About Viridis

Viridis Plumbing & Heating is run by Phil, a plumbing and heating engineer based in Liverpool. His work is split between social housing and private homes, and nearly all of it comes through word of mouth. This site puts his finished jobs in one place, so new customers can see the standard before they pick up the phone.
