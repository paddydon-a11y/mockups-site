About Newstyle Builders

Newstyle Builders Ltd is Lee and a team of experienced, certified tradesmen with over 30 years in the building industry. Working from our unit on Thurne Way in Liverpool, we take homes and commercial buildings from first drawing to finished build, and plan every project around your budget.
