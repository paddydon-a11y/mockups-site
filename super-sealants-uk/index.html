About Super Sealants UK

Super Sealants UK is a team of NVQ level 2 and IPAF trained sealant applicators based in Great Harwood, near Blackburn. We seal concrete joints and apply mastic sealants on commercial, industrial and domestic projects. Our dust-free method for cleaning out and sealing internal saw cuts comes at no extra cost, so other trades can carry on working while we seal.
