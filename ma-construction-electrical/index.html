About MA Construction & Electrical

The build and the wiring, one number

MA Construction & Electrical Ltd is a Stockport building and electrical contractor working mostly on interiors: renovations, fit-outs, feature ceilings and lighting for homeowners and commercial clients within about 75 miles of Stockport. A core team of three, with trusted subcontractors brought in as the job needs. Because the building side and the electrical side are the same firm, the first fix is planned with the plastering and the lighting is designed with the ceiling, not squeezed in afterwards.
