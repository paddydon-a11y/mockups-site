About

One team, every trade.

A&M Interiors design, build and finish work across London, from a loft conversion or an extension down to a single bathroom. Electrics, plumbing, plastering, tiling, flooring and decorating are all handled by our own team, so there is one number to ring and nobody waiting on anybody else to turn up.

Floors sheeted, dust controlled, waste gone at the end of every day, and the keys handed back with the place spotless.

How we work

From first look to handover.

STEP 01

We come and look

You call or send photos on WhatsApp, we come out and measure up, and you tell us what you want the finished room to do. Nothing is chargeable at that point.

STEP 02

Design and fixed quote

Drawings where the job needs them, a written specification of what is included, and a fixed price so there are no numbers landing halfway through.

STEP 03

One team on site

Strip out, structural work, electrics, plumbing, plastering, tiling and decorating in the right order, with floors protected and waste cleared as we go.

STEP 04

Walk round and clean

We snag the job with you room by room, put right anything you are not happy with, and hand the keys back with the place cleaned.
