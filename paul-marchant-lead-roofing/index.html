About Paul

A craftsman, not a general roofer

Lead is Paul's trade. From seafront domes to church gutters and one-off finials, he cuts, dresses and lead-welds every job by hand, then backs it up with general roofing when the rest of the roof needs doing. Based in Brighton, happy to travel across the South East for the right work.
