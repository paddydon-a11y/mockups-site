About Bells Carpentry

David Bell founded Bells Carpentry in Milton Keynes in 2006. Twenty years on, the company works for housebuilders, developers and main contractors on new-build and commercial projects, and for homeowners on bespoke oak and domestic work. One team takes a project from first fix through to second fix.
