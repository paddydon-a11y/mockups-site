About MGF Contracts Ltd

MGF Contracts Ltd is a commercial and industrial fit-out contractor based in Essex. We work for businesses, landlords and facilities managers, delivering office and warehouse projects with our own project management and an established network of specialist trade subcontractors. One team is responsible for the programme, the standard of finish and the handover.

How we deliver a project

Site survey and brief

We visit the premises, review drawings and agree the scope with the client or their consultant.

Quotation and programme

A priced scope of works with a programme showing each trade and key dates.

Managed delivery

Our project manager coordinates every trade on site and reports progress to the client.

Snagging and handover

Works are inspected, snags closed out and the space handed over ready for use.
