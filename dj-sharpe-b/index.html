A Small Family Business

D J Sharpe is a family haulage business run from Vista Road in Newton-le-Willows. A small team of operator drivers loads, chains and drives every job, from tracked dumpers to potato harvesters and combines. The yard is minutes from the M6 and M62, so loads go out in every direction.
