Automated sorting technology

Our Kenyon line pairs an Eggersmann Forus F38 slow-speed shredder with ZenRobotics automated pickers. The shredder sizes mixed waste so the robots can pick it efficiently, which lets us handle higher volumes through the working day, recover more material and send less to landfill.

  • Eggersmann Forus F38 primary shredder
  • ZenRobotics automated picking
  • Higher recovery, lower landfill tax exposure
