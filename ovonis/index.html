About Ovonis

Ovonis is a specialist water, drainage and leak detection company based in Birmingham. We investigate water and drainage problems for property owners and professional teams, explain the findings and what remains uncertain, and carry out suitable repairs where the work is within our scope.

  • Homeowners
  • Property and facilities managers
  • Insurers and surveyors
  • Housing providers
  • Contractors and developers

Our investigation process

We choose methods suited to the problem and the site, including CCTV inspection, acoustic listening, tracer gas, thermal imaging and drainage tracing.

Investigate

Site attendance with the right equipment for the symptoms.

Identify

Locate the defect, leak or blockage and its cause.

Report

Findings recorded with imagery and any uncertainty stated.

Recommend

Access, maintenance or repair options agreed with you.

Repair

Suitable repairs carried out and the completed work recorded.
