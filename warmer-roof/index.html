About Warmer Roof

Every Warmer Roof is prefabricated in our 60,000 sq ft factory on the Marston Trading Estate in Frome, built to ISO 9001 and structurally approved by independent engineers. Kits leave on a five day turnaround on our own lorries, and our 5,000 sq ft trade showroom next to the production plant is open to installers by appointment.
