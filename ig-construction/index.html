IGWho We Are

Structural expertise, meticulous detail

IG Construction is a friendly, professional building company with 20 years of experience across Carmarthenshire. Based in Trimsaran, near Kidwelly, we handle extensions, renovations, conversions, new builds and groundworks across Llanelli, Burry Port, Swansea and Cross Hands. From first chat to final handover, we keep things clear, tidy and on schedule.
