About Mech NW

Founded in 2020, Mech NW is an M&E contractor with over 30 years of combined experience across residential, commercial and industrial sectors. All three of our managers worked their way up from apprenticeships. Based in the North West, we operate nationwide and are a trusted partner for main contractors seeking reliability, expertise and results.
