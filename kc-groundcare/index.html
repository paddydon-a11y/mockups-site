About KC GroundCare

KC GroundCare Limited is a family-run grounds maintenance and landscaping business based in Kirkintilloch. We look after retail and commercial sites, common ground, rental properties and private gardens across Glasgow and Central Scotland, on regular maintenance contracts and one-off jobs, with more than ten years in the trade behind the work.
