About

C.S. Plastering is a Stoke-on-Trent plastering firm covering everywhere within about an hour of the Potteries. From a single patch repair to a full house, every job gets the same finish: flat walls, sharp corners, dust sheets down and a tidy sweep-up before we leave. Call or WhatsApp and you speak to the plasterer, not a call centre.
