About MAL Plumbing and Heating

MAL Plumbing and Heating is a local, independent team run by Mark and Graham from Birkdale, established in 1995. The black van with the orange flame is a familiar sight across Southport and West Lancashire, fitting boilers, heating systems and bathrooms. Every quote is free, with no obligation.

Gas Safe registered. The Gas Safe Register is the official UK list of engineers legally allowed to work on gas boilers, fires and cookers.
