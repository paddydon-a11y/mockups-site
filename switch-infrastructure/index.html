Capability

What that looks like on a project

Survey

Our in house survey teams provide topographic, geotechnical and site investigation services. The survey offering covers Track, OLE, Civils and Structures in line with our design and construction services, which lets us offer a turn key solution and seamless integration throughout your project.

Design

Our multidiscipline design offering is delivered by in house design teams and partners. We pride ourselves on the quality of our outputs and our engineering capability to find the best solution in the most challenging environments. Designers interface with delivery teams throughout, so safety and constructability are front and centre.

Monitoring

A one stop shop for your monitoring needs, from developing requirements, provision of equipment and site preparation, through to installation of the chosen solution and monitoring either remotely or on site. We have access to the latest equipment and we analyse and report on the data captured.

PMO and Advisory

Our PMO offering is structured around risk management, focusing on stability and high performing teams within Safety, Quality, Programme and Commercial. Continual improvement is achieved through data capture, analysis and implementation, using an Intelligent Control Platform to integrate and manage all aspects of the project.

Construction

Full project lifecycle support, from early involvement where we support clients with value engineering, system integration and efficient planning, through to right first time delivery. We install, test and commission, and offer maintenance and inspection services through the construction arm of the business.

Market Entry

We support SMEs developing clean technologies with market strategy, market knowledge and a network built to accelerate commercialisation. We work with these companies through the development phase, support market entry, and once established we step away and hand over to business as usual.
