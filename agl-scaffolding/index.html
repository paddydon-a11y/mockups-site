About AGL

Built on new-build sites

AGL Scaffold is a Sussex scaffolding contractor that grew up on housing developments. We run our own yard, lorries and telehandler, with a site division for developments and a street division for town work. Developers get one number, a programme that matches their build, and scaffolds that look as good as they perform.
