About

Fifty years, same trade

Keith Aitken has been a bricklayer for over fifty years and still works on the tools himself. A lot of the work these days comes through insurance companies: storm damage, impact damage, cracked and settled walls that need cutting out and rebuilding to match what was there before.

The rest is what it has always been. Garden walls, piers, chimney stacks, extensions and repointing for homeowners across South London and Surrey.
