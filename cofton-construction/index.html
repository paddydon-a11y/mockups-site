About Cofton Construction

Cofton Construction Ltd is a residential building contractor based in Kings Norton, Birmingham, established in 2016. We build new homes, extensions and refurbishments for homeowners, landlords and developers across Birmingham and the West Midlands, and work with main contractors on subcontract packages.
