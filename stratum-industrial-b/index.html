Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply IRATA rope access technicians?

Yes. Stratum Industrial is an IRATA International member and supplies IRATA rope access teams for inspection, maintenance, installation, coatings and challenging access works.

Can you provide short-term labour as well as full project delivery?

Yes. We supply black trades and skilled labour for short-term requirements and deliver complete packages including planning, RAMS, supervision and QA/QC.

Do you provide coating inspection and QA/QC documentation?

Our coatings division is led by an ICorr Level 3 Paint Inspector. We carry out inspection, thickness and application checks and provide full photographic and written records for traceability.

Which accreditations do you hold?

We are ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certified, Constructionline Gold, SSIP registered, an IRATA member, a Building Safety Group member and hold Bronze membership of the Supply Chain Sustainability School.

Where in the UK do you work?

We are based in Teesside and work on construction, infrastructure, energy and offshore projects nationwide.
