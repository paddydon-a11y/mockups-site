About Propel Electrical

Propel Electrical was set up in 2018 by two London electricians, Josh Ahmet and Scott Perkins. Starting as a pair, the company now runs several vans across London every day and works for councils, housing associations, main contractors and private clients. Most of our work comes from repeat clients and referrals.
