About LDS

A family run groundworks business

LDS Contracting works across Cambridgeshire and well beyond it. Whether it is a large construction project or a small landscaping job, we make sure it is finished efficiently and safely. We run our own plant, bring in extra labour and operators when a project calls for it, and take on everything from farm and commercial sites through to private driveways.
