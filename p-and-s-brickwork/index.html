About P&S Brickwork

P&S Brickwork was established by Paul and Stuart, two experienced bricklayers who each ran their own bricklaying companies for many years before joining forces. With 40 years of combined experience and two teams brought together, the business delivers demanding programmes on projects of every size without compromising on quality.
