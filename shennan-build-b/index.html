About Shennan Build

Shennan Build is a general building contractor run by Daniel Shennan from Lydiate, on the northern edge of Liverpool. Since opening in 2012 the business has worked mainly on homes, building, extending and renovating them across Merseyside and the North West, with every project carried out meticulously and with integrity.
