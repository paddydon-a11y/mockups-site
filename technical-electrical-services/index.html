About TES

Established in Blackpool. Trusted across the North.

Technical and Electrical Services Ltd has been an electrical contractor since 1988, working for councils, housing providers, the NHS, main contractors and commercial clients across the north of England and Cumbria. Qualified engineers, supervisors trained in health and safety, first-aid trained staff, and a complete service with the minimum of fuss.
