About
Light Speed

Light Speed Utilities Ltd is a utility and civil engineering contractor based in Ellesmere Port. Our directional drilling teams install ducts and services under roads, verges and live sites, and the same company digs and shores the pits, lays the cable and reinstates the ground afterwards. We take on work across the North West and nationwide.

Directional drilling process

Pilot bore

The drill head is steered from the launch pit along the planned line and tracked from the surface.

Ream

On the way back the bore is opened up to size with a reamer, ready for the duct.

Pullback

The duct or pipe is pulled through to the launch pit and both pits are reinstated.
