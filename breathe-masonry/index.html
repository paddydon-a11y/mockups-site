Why the name

Traditional lime work. A wall that can breathe.

We specialise in traditional lime work and building walls, wet walls or dry stone. Cornish granite and cob were never meant to be sealed in cement, and lime lets the wet out again. We also plaster inside and out, have our own painting team for a freshen up, and take on landscaping from a patio to an outdoor kitchen. Competent, competitive on price, and we will consider any work we feel capable of doing.
