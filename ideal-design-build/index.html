About us

Family run since 2014 and based in Emerson Park, we work across Essex and East London. The team that draws the job is the team that builds it: brickwork, carpentry, electrics and plumbing all sit in house, so there is one number to ring from the first drawing to the final clean.

Alongside the domestic work we fit out shops and commercial units, including national convenience brands, so we are used to working to a fixed opening date.
