FAQs

Is Adjudicate right for you?

What is adjudication?

Adjudication is a statutory legal process used to resolve disputes in the construction industry quickly and cost-effectively. It is a right under the Construction Act, and the decision is binding unless and until it is overturned by litigation or arbitration.

What types of disputes can adjudication resolve?

A wide range of construction disputes, including non-payment, variations, delays, defects and final account disagreements.

Do I need legal representation for adjudication?

Legal representation is not mandatory, but having an expert on your side significantly increases your chances of success. An experienced adjudication specialist guides you through the process, helps you prepare strong evidence and makes sure your case is presented effectively, saving time, stress and potentially costly mistakes.

How long does the adjudication process take?

Typically around 28 days from the date the dispute is referred to an adjudicator. That can be extended by 14 days with the referring party's consent, or longer if both sides agree.

What are the costs involved?

Costs vary with the complexity of the case. We always offer totally transparent fees with no hidden costs, and the first call is free.
