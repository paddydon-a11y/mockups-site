About us

Builders with a joiner's eye

PJ Joinery is a Croydon building team covering South East London and everywhere within 30 miles. Most of our work comes from recommendations: extensions, renovations and new builds where the plastering, plumbing and electrics are handled in-house, and the staircases, wardrobes and shelving come out of our own workshop.
