Questions

Before you book in

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes. We visit your home, look at the space and the existing plumbing and electrics, then send a detailed written quote with no obligation.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

A typical full bathroom renovation takes 10 to 15 working days: rip-out, first fix and waterproofing in week one, tiling and second fix in week two. You get a timeline with your quote.

Can you fit a kitchen I have bought myself?

Yes. If you have bought from Howdens, Wren, Magnet or another supplier, we fit it and handle the plumbing, sockets, plastering, tiling and flooring around it.

Can you handle large format tiles?

We specialise in them. Tiles of 600mm and above are laid with levelling systems for flat floors and straight lines.

Do you offer guarantees on your work?

Yes, all our workmanship is guaranteed. The warranty period depends on the work and materials, and we go through it at quote stage.
