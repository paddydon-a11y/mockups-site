Case Studies

The works register

Ten recent projects, with the scope of work exactly as it was delivered. Open a row to read the overview, the scope and the outcome.

Overview

To design, fabricate and install internal steel access platforms within an existing dry well, ensuring safe and efficient access for maintenance and operations.

  • Supply chain collaboration. J Frost Civils handled the groundwork, project management and site supervision, overseeing every aspect and ensuring subcontracted works were completed on schedule.
  • Fabrication. Coordinated with our expert fabrication partner so steel works were fabricated and installed to specifications approved by both J Frost Civils and the client.
  • Inspection and testing. Thorough inspections at every stage to ensure compliance with safety regulations and project specifications.
  • Load testing and certification. Performed load testing and provided certification confirming the platforms safely support the intended weight.
  • Documentation. On completion all documentation, including load testing certification and inspection reports, was provided at handover.

Outcome

Safe, secure and accessible steel platforms within the dry well, offering reliable access for employees and contractors to perform routine maintenance and operational works, with full compliance and documentation.

Overview

Installation of four new concrete plinths to support a blower kiosk and internal assets. The work was carried out within a tight schedule due to the commissioning and decommissioning required between each plinth's construction.

  • Site assessment. Detailed assessment with the client and design team to confirm precise dimensions and alignment with the plans, with site utilities identified and highlighted before excavation.
  • Formwork. Excavated to the required depth and constructed two tiered formwork to specified dimensions, securely positioned and reinforced with rebar to withstand the pour.
  • Concrete. Poured C35 grade concrete, used a vibrating poker to eliminate air pockets and achieve proper compaction, then levelled and smoothed to a uniform finish.
  • Curing. Standard curing practices followed for strength and durability, with formwork carefully removed and visually inspected for imperfections.
  • Quality control. Cube test results issued to the client and a final joint inspection carried out to confirm all specifications and standards were met.

Outcome

Four robust, level concrete plinths providing a structurally sound and stable foundation for the blower kiosk and internal assets, with long term performance and durability for critical site operations.

Overview

Design and construction of a safe and efficient entry system for a wet well, enabling maintenance, inspection and the removal of assets, built to meet industry standards and ensure long term functionality and safety.

  • Planning and design. A detailed plan covering access points, safety features and compliance with industry standards, designed to allow seamless access to internal assets for replacement, repairs and inspections.
  • Safety measures. Comprehensive temporary safety protocols established for working within the wet well, including an internal safety deck to protect existing assets during construction.
  • Construction. Built a new 9 inch brick chamber to house the entry system, incorporating all existing services without disruption.
  • Access cover. Designed, fabricated and installed a custom access cover featuring a fall arrest safety grill, meeting industry specification.
  • Handover. Thorough inspections and a full handover including demonstrations and final checks.

Outcome

A secure and functional entry system giving safe, reliable access to the wet well, with enhanced safety features such as the fall arrest grill and a durable, compliant design built for long term use.

Overview

Excavation and construction of a new chamber with a concrete base, blockwork structure and associated pipework replacements, delivered to a detailed plan and a tight deadline.

  • Locate and mark. CAT scanned the works area to locate and mark services, then marked out and cut for the new chamber to the required dimensions.
  • Excavation. Excavated in layers to a depth of 3.2m, ensuring clearance under the existing pipework, with spoil removed into roll on roll off skips.
  • Temporary works. Supplied and installed edge protection and an escape davit for personnel safety during the excavation.
  • Base and chamber. Installed a 150mm concrete base with one mesh reinforcement and a 350mm wide sump, then constructed a 40N blockwork chamber with 2.5m x 2.5m internal opening, reinforced with 16mm steel bars tied into the base to a final height 100mm below ground level.
  • Infill and finishing. C30 concrete infill to the block recesses, bolt down bollards finished in black with yellow stripes, and two core holes drilled to 1.8m over existing pipework for replacement.

Outcome

A robust concrete base and blockwork chamber meeting the specified dimensions and safety requirements, with safe, clear access for future pipework replacement and maintenance, and full compliance with health and safety protocols throughout.

Overview

A programme focused on improving site safety, structural integrity and accessibility, through a series of targeted actions to ensure durability, functionality and compliance with safety standards.

  • De-vegetation and perimeter. Cleared vegetation around the exterior perimeter, with minor repairs and strengthening to the existing chain link fencing.
  • Concrete and handrails. Supplied and installed a 2.4m x 400mm x 400mm concrete extension as a foundation for a 10m tubular handrail and a perimeter handrail with D-returns for edge protection and a safe working area.
  • Access improvements. Constructed and poured access steps to the concrete slab, ensuring safe entry and exit.
  • Ladder replacement. Safely removed the existing wet well ladder using a bosun's chair and reciprocating saw, then supplied and installed a replacement Techno Cover mounted to accommodate internal asset height and slab thickness.
  • Kiosk repairs. Fitted new kiosk doors, repaired the rear wall, repainted the external surface and applied mastic to seal the joint between the cabinet and the concrete plinth.

Outcome

A safer, more functional and visually improved site. Tubular handrails, durable access steps and newly installed covers and doors ensure long term usability while meeting high safety standards.

Overview

Addressing a significant safety hazard caused by corrosion in a valve chamber. The scope included replacing three corroded covers, enhancing site safety, and upgrading access and ventilation systems to meet water industry standards.

  • Removal. Safely removed three corroded covers and stored them in an unhindered location.
  • Replacement covers. Supplied and installed two heavy duty split D400 covers at 900mm x 600mm for the valve chamber.
  • Custom cover. One custom manufactured galvanised retractable steel cover at 1,470mm x 750mm designed to water industry standards, with a lock back hinge and safety grille, a recessed aperture with a hasp for padlock compatibility, and two doors galvanised post fabrication for durability.
  • Access. Installed Saxon buff slabs to create a durable hardstand for accessing the covers, with soil reinstated around the new slabs to eliminate trip hazards.
  • Ventilation. Removed the existing wet well vent and installed a new coupling and pipe extension raising the vent to a 500mm finished height, completed with a new mushroom vent and secure fixings.

Outcome

The corrosion hazard resolved with a secure and accessible solution: heavy duty and custom manufactured covers, hazard elimination and upgraded access, and compliance with water industry standards for ventilation and cover design.

Overview

A comprehensive project focused on the safe removal of asbestos roofing, structural repairs and repainting to enhance both safety and appearance, executed with strict adherence to safety regulations and industry standards.

  • Preparation. Segregated the work area to ensure safety and containment, and supplied and installed a roof access platform to facilitate safe operations.
  • Asbestos removal. Removed asbestos bitumen roofing in compliance with health and safety guidelines, safely stored all coping removed from the roof, and supplied the client with all relevant documentation.
  • Roof repairs. Repaired concrete roof joints for structural integrity, applied two layers of torch on felt including upstands for waterproofing, and reinstated all coping stones on a new bed of mortar.
  • Internal repairs. Removed all render from the interior roof, sealed internal cracks in the ceiling, and applied a coat of masonry paint.
  • Handover. Removed all waste generated during the project and provided completion photographs alongside the asbestos removal documentation.

Outcome

Safe removal and documentation of hazardous asbestos materials, durable and watertight roofing with structural repairs and new felt installation, and improved appearance and functionality of the interior ceiling.

Overview

Construction of a robust dividing wall to enhance site functionality, completed efficiently with an emphasis on structural stability and precision in material handling and installation.

  • Preparation. Arranged delivery of all necessary materials and skip to site, ensuring placement in a safe and accessible location.
  • Anchoring. Drilled 18mm tie in bars into the top of the existing 250mm wide wall for secure anchoring.
  • Wall construction. Supplied and installed heavy dense hollow blocks to construct the dividing wall at 1500mm high and 5070mm long, applying mortar between blocks and inserting drilled rods into the recesses for additional stability.
  • Reinforcement. On achieving the required height, hand mixed C30 concrete on site and filled the recesses of the blocks for enhanced strength and structural integrity.
  • Handover. Cleared all waste and provided completion photographs showcasing the finished wall.

Outcome

A durable and stable structure reinforced with tie in bars and concrete filling, executed cleanly and professionally, leaving the site in optimal condition.

Overview

Installation of a 200m duct run with draw pits, providing the infrastructure for cable routing. Meticulously planned and executed to meet both functional and safety standards.

  • Route. Conducted a site visit to finalise the ducting route in collaboration with the client.
  • Excavation. Excavated to a depth of 800mm, performing CAT scans every 150mm to ensure safe working conditions and avoid underground obstructions.
  • Duct installation. Supplied and installed a 200m duct run using 150mm ducting along the agreed route, with 200mm of sand bedding over the ducting for protection followed by marker tape to indicate the cable route.
  • Draw pits. Supplied and installed two draw pits made from precast concrete sections at 750mm x 450mm, fitted with lightweight frames and covers for accessibility and durability.
  • Reinstatement. Backfilled the trench using as dug materials to restore the site to its original condition, installed a draw cord through the entire route, and removed all waste from site.

Outcome

A robust and properly installed ducting system ready for cable routing, accessible draw pits built to durable specifications, and a tidy, professionally finished site.

Overview

A project focused on improving site safety by installing new access steps and handrails and making adjustments to existing infrastructure. The enhancements were designed to blend seamlessly with the surroundings while ensuring functionality and compliance with safety standards.

  • Vegetation clearance. Cleared minor vegetation to create space for the installation of new access steps.
  • Access steps. Constructed access steps using H blocks reinforced with steel rods and filled with concrete for stability, then supplied and installed Saxon slabs in a buff finish for the step tops.
  • Discretion. Ensured the steps were discreetly concealed by surrounding plantation to minimise visibility from nearby houses.
  • Edge protection. Supplied and installed a two tier handrail and kick plate, surface mounted at the top of the wet well.
  • Kiosk works. Removed and reinstated the fixings for the kiosk ensuring proper levelling, prepared and rendered the existing kiosk base, and applied a fresh bead of mastic to seal it.

Outcome

A safer and more functional site with durable, stable access steps that integrate well with the surrounding environment, improved edge protection, and a level, well sealed kiosk base. Safety improvements delivered with thoughtful design and attention to detail.
