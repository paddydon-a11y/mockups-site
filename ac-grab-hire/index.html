About AC Grab Hire

Adam has nearly 20 years in the industry. AC Grab Hire Ltd runs a 16 tonne Volvo grab lorry from The Mill Business Park on Maidstone Road, Ashford, for builders, groundworkers, landscapers and homeowners across Kent and London. As much of what we collect as possible is recycled.
