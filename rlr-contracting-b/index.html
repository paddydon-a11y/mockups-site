About RLR Contracting

RLR Contracting is a Cambridge roofing and cladding contractor with a team of 14, working on homes, commercial units and farm buildings. Specialist experience built up on other contractors' sites now goes into full supply and fit packages and contracts taken on directly, with one team responsible from the first survey to the finished roof.
