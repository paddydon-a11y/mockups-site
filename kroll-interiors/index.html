About Kroll Interiors

A London tiling contractor with a national client base

Kroll Interiors is a London-based business with a strong heritage of delivering superior tiling solutions to private and commercial customers in London and the UK.

Starting small and rapidly achieving a national client base, today we are one of the largest UK tiling contractors, specialising in high-quality wall and floor tiling and stone work for the construction industry.
