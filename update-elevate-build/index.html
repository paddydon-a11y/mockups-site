About Us

Everything under one contract

Update & Elevate Build is a London building contractor with a team of around 28, working across most of London, Essex and roughly fifty miles out from Chiswick.

The point of the business is simple. Groundworks, structure, first fix, plastering, second fix and finishes are handled in house, so you are not sourcing five different trades and standing between them when something slips.
