About Collins Roofing

Collins Roofing NW Ltd is a family run roofing firm based at Acorn Works on Jackson Street, St Helens. Locally owned and operated since 1975, we work on homes and commercial buildings across the North West, and a major part of our work comes through recommendation. We offer 10 year insurance backed guarantees.

CORC Approved Member

The Confederation of Roofing Contractors is a UK roofing trade association, established in 1985, that issues insurance backed guarantees for its members' work.

Trading Standards Approved

Buy With Confidence is run by local authority Trading Standards services. Member businesses are checked by Trading Standards and agree to trade fairly.
