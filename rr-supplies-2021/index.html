Orders and delivery questions

Looking for something in particular? These are the products people search for most.

Do you have a minimum order value?

Yes. The minimum order is £10, not including any delivery charge.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across the UK mainland. Delivery is free to homes and businesses in the Ilkeston area of Derbyshire, and free anywhere on the mainland when your order is over £50. We cannot deliver to Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man, the Scottish islands or other offshore areas.

How long does delivery take?

Local orders usually arrive in 1 to 2 business days, with same day delivery in Ilkeston when you order before 10am. The rest of the UK mainland is usually 2 to 3 business days.

Can I change or cancel my order after I have placed it?

Yes. Send us a message using the contact form and we will reply within 1 to 2 business days. Cancellations need to be requested within 5 hours of purchase.

Can I return a product?

Yes. If your order arrives damaged, or does not arrive, contact us within 14 days of delivery and send items back within 30 days. Buyers cover the return postage.

How can I pay?

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express at the checkout.

Question about an order? Send us a message

Use the form for anything about your order, a cancellation, a refund, delivery or a return. We reply within 1 to 2 business days.
