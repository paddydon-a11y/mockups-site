Health and Safety

When working at height, the safety of our team, our customers and anyone near the project is our main focus. The same checks run through every job, whether it is a single house or a ship in dry dock.

  1. Site Survey

    Every site is visited and surveyed before any scaffold is planned.

  2. Risk Assessed

    Risks to the team, the client and the public assessed before work starts.

  3. CISRS Scaffolders

    Built by trained, carded scaffolders to current guidance and legislation.

  4. Signed Off

    Every scaffold signed off and fully insured before it is handed over.

CHAS accredited. CHAS assesses a contractor's health and safety policies and records against recognised standards, so main contractors, councils and schools can see the checks have been passed.

CISRS registered scaffolders. CISRS is the construction industry's scaffolder training scheme. A CISRS card shows a scaffolder has been trained and assessed for the work they carry out.
