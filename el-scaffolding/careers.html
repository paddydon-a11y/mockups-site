Scaffolding Jobs in Liverpool

E&L Scaffolding is always keen to hear from scaffolders, advanced scaffolders, labourers and trainees who want to work on commercial, marine, industrial and domestic sites across Liverpool and the North West.

Roles We Recruit For

Scaffolders

CISRS Scaffolder card

Tube and fitting scaffolds on homes, shops, schools, industrial sites and docks, working as part of a small team.

Advanced Scaffolders

CISRS Advanced card

Complex and designed scaffolds for commercial, industrial and marine projects, including access in shipyards and dry docks.

Labourers and Trainees

CSCS or CISRS Labourer card

Supporting the team on site, loading and unloading, and learning the trade with a view to working towards a CISRS card.

Working at E&L Scaffolding

  • Safety firstEvery site is surveyed and risk assessed before a tube goes up, and every scaffold is signed off before it is handed over.
  • Varied workCommercial, marine, industrial and domestic scaffolding, on homes, shops, schools, factories and docks.
  • Liverpool basedHead office on Love Lane, with work across Merseyside and the North West.
  • CHAS accreditedA contractor whose health and safety policies and records have been independently checked.
