About Taylor Warehouse Systems

Taylor Warehouse Systems is a pallet racking installation company based in Dallas-Fort Worth and run by owner Ben Taylor. Ben brings a background in industrial construction and managing subcontractors to every warehouse project, from new installations and expansions to reconfigurations, repairs and inspections, in Texas and across the country.

Warehouse racking

Racking systems

  • Selective pallet rackDirect access to every pallet, the standard for most warehouses.
  • Cantilever rackArms in place of beams for pipe, lumber and long loads.
  • Push-back rackPallets stored several deep on carts, loaded from one aisle.
  • Drive-in rackHigh-density lanes for large quantities of the same product.
  • Mezzanine-supported systemsRacking that carries a raised floor to add working space.

Safety and standards

  • Installation and inspection work carried out with OSHA workplace requirements and RMI racking guidance in mind.
  • Inspections focused on warehouse safety: uprights, beams, bracing and anchors.
  • Industrial construction and project management experience on every job.
