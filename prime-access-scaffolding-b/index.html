About Prime Access Scaffolding

Prime Access Scaffolding NW Ltd is a scaffolding contractor based in Blacon, Chester. We erect, alter and strike scaffolding for homeowners, roofers, builders and developers across Chester, Cheshire and North Wales, with temporary roofs and edge protection where the job needs them. Every scaffold is planned around the work going on it, and every quote is free.
