About Eco Classrooms and Nurseries

We started Eco Classrooms and Nurseries because schools, nurseries and community spaces deserve better than standard, out-of-the-box buildings. Every project is designed around the people who use it, with thoughtful layouts, natural materials and lower running costs.

Our Process

  1. 1

    Understanding your needs, onsite

    We visit your site to understand your requirements and what is possible.

  2. 2

    Design and planning

    Tailored designs with our architect, plus planning applications and building regulations.

  3. 3

    Offsite construction

    Your building is manufactured offsite in SIPs while groundworks are prepared.

  4. 4

    Fast, efficient assembly

    Delivered and assembled on site in as little as 4 to 6 weeks.

  5. 5

    Handover and aftercare

    A full walkthrough, clear warranties and support long after handover.
